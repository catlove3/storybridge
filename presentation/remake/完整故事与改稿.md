## 原稿

《不是我的成绩》——虚构短剧，七场

【S01 她没去的考试】
高三学生小宁想学画画，父亲却认定只有名校才有出路。高考前小宁摔伤住院，错过考试。一个升学中介私下对父亲说：“人不用到，成绩我们安排。”父亲以为有特殊补考名额，交了钱。小宁问到底怎么考，中介没有回答。

【S02 一份不属于她的成绩】
中介发来一份写着小宁名字的高考成绩单，还有一张模糊的入场核验照片，催父亲赶紧办升学材料。小宁说：“可那两天我一直在医院。”父亲反复看着高分，劝她先别问，错过就没有机会了。她把成绩单和照片都存进手机。

【S03 朋友留下的线索】
小岚来还毕业纪念册，她帮同学整理过本班的考试材料，留下了一份考场座位表。她指着照片里一处模糊的座位号说：“我记得这个位置坐的不是你。”小宁没有立刻下结论，约她把原始材料带来核对。纪念册里夹着小宁画了很久的自画像。

【S04 再交一笔钱】
中介说升学材料需要进一步核验，让父亲再交一笔“协调费”。他暗示一旦有人追问，孩子的前途就完了。父亲又准备借钱，小宁按住他的手机：“先查清楚，那份成绩到底是谁的。”

【S05 她决定拆穿】
小岚带来原始座位表，两人比对中介的入场照片和小宁的住院记录。照片里的考生坐在另一名同学的座位，考试当天小宁仍躺在病床上。父女这才确认：所谓特殊名额是让另一个人替她参加高考。小宁把证据存好，对父亲说：“你给我买的未来，连里面的人都不是我。”

【S06 放弃那条捷径】
父亲承认自己不敢面对女儿错过考试，只想抓住一个名校名额。父女保存中介承诺、付款记录和原始材料，主动向相关学校说明情况，停止使用假成绩，愿意配合核查。父亲把借款页面关掉，把画册推回女儿面前：“那我们从你真正会的事情开始。”

【S07 她自己的名字】
过了一段时间，小宁用自己的画申请艺术方向课程，拿到了作品面试邀请。她把邀请和那张自画像放在父亲面前：“这一次，上面的名字和作品，都是我的。”父亲第一次认真翻完她的毕业纪念册。

## 朴素全文改编：完整英语结果

# NOT MY RECORD

## A Short Drama in Seven Scenes

### Adapted for a U.S. Setting

---

**ADAPTATION NOTES (for production):**

- **Setting:** A mid-sized American city, present day. All universities, clubs, and regattas are fictional.
- **Xiaoning → NINA ZHAO**, 17, a high school senior who loves to draw. She has never rowed.
- **Father → DAVID ZHAO**, late 40s, first-generation immigrant, works long hours, believes an Ivy League acceptance is the only proof his family has made it.
- **Xiaolan → JULES PARK**, Nina's classmate and friend, a student aide in the athletics office.
- **The Broker → RICKY SALAZAR**, a "college admissions consultant" who promises athletic recruitment slots.
- **The scam:** Ricky fabricates a rowing résumé for Nina — a fake club registration, a Photoshopped crew-team photo with Nina's face pasted onto another rower's body, and a forged "Regional Regatta Entry Confirmation" bearing her name. The promise: "You don't need to show up. We arrange the recruit slot."
- **What Nina misses:** A rowing recruit showcase (not an exam) — she's hospitalized after a fall.
- **Key evidence chain:** Jules brings the real club roster and seat assignments; cross-referenced with Ricky's photo and Nina's hospital records, it proves Nina was never in the boat.
- **No magic database, no certificate that guarantees admission.** The fraud is exposed through documents, photos, and a hospital log — all filmable.

## S01 — THE SHOWCASE SHE MISSED

**INT. HOSPITAL ROOM — DAY**

NINA ZHAO, 17, lies in a hospital bed, right leg in a cast, sketchbook open on her lap. A half-finished drawing of a rowing shell on the water. She's not happy with it.

Her father, DAVID ZHAO, stands by the window, phone pressed to his ear. He's been on hold for a while.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (into phone)
> Yes, I understand the showcase was yesterday. She was in a hospital bed. There has to be —

He listens. His jaw tightens.

<center>NINA</center>
> Dad. It's fine.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (covering the phone)
> It's not fine. This was the one day the coaches were going to be there. The one day.

He hangs up. Stares out the window.

<center>NINA</center>
> I never rowed anyway. I draw.

<center>DAVID</center>
> You draw. And you'll draw at a good school. But you get into a good school through a door. This was a door.

The door opens. RICKY SALAZAR, 40s, polo shirt, tablet under his arm, steps in like he owns the hallway.

<center>RICKY</center>
> Mr. Zhao? Ricky Salazar. We spoke on the phone.

David shakes his hand too eagerly.

<center>RICKY</center>
> (glancing at Nina)
> This is the athlete?

<center>NINA</center>
> I'm not an athlete.

<center>RICKY</center>
> (smiling)
> That's what the file says. And the file is what schools read.

He turns to David, lowers his voice — but not enough.

<center>RICKY</center>
> Here's the situation. The showcase is done. But recruit slots? Those are arranged. You don't need to be in the boat. You need to be in the paperwork.

<center>DAVID</center>
> What does that mean?

<center>RICKY</center>
> It means the door is still open. But it closes fast.

Nina watches them. Her pencil stops moving.

<center>NINA</center>
> How do I try out?

Ricky doesn't answer her. He's already scrolling his tablet, talking to David.

<center>RICKY</center>
> I'll need a retainer to start the file. We move quickly.

<center>NINA</center>
> (louder)
> How do I try out?

Ricky looks at her. A beat. Then back to David.

<center>RICKY</center>
> She's got spirit. Coaches love spirit.

He doesn't answer the question.

## S02 — A RECORD THAT ISN'T HERS

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — NIGHT**

A small kitchen. Dishes drying. David sits at the table, laptop open. Nina limps in on crutches.

David's phone buzzes. He opens an email. His face changes.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Nina. Look at this.

He turns the laptop toward her. On screen:

- A **"Regional Regatta Entry Confirmation"** — Nina Zhao's name, a date, a boat number.

<center>NINA</center>
> That's not me.

<center>DAVID</center>
> It has your name.

<center>NINA</center>
> Dad. Those two days? I was in the hospital. You were there. You slept in that chair.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (quiet)
> I know.

He keeps looking at the confirmation. The date. The boat number.

<center>DAVID</center>
> But look at this. A regional regatta. That's a real event. If a coach sees this —

<center>NINA</center>
> Sees what? A photo of someone else's body with my face on it?

<center>DAVID</center>
> Don't say that.

<center>DAVID</center>

## S07 — HER OWN NAME

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — DAY — WEEKS LATER**

David comes in, holding the mail. He stops.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Nina.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Just an interview?

<center>NINA</center>

<center>NINA</center>

<center>DAVID</center>
> This is good.

<center>NINA</center>
> I know.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I mean it.

<center>NINA</center>
> I know you do.

A college admissions RECRUITER speaks to the Father in private.

RECRUITER
She doesn't have to be there. We'll arrange the recruit slot.

The Father believes there's a special channel and pays.

XIAONING
How exactly do I join the recruiting?

The Recruiter doesn't answer.

XIAONING
But I was in the hospital those two days.

FATHER
Don't ask. If we miss this, there's no second chance.

### S03 The Clue a Friend Left Behind

XIAOLAN comes by to return the yearbook. She used to help rowing club classmates organize recruiting materials, and she leaves behind a real roster and seating chart.

XIAOLAN
I remember this seat. It wasn't you.

XIAONING
Bring me the original materials. Let's compare.

### S04 Another Payment

RECRUITER

XIAONING

### S05 She Decides to Expose It

Xiaolan brings the rowing club's original roster and seating chart. The two spread the recruiter's crew photo on the table and compare.

The girl whose face was pasted with Xiaoning's is sitting in another rower's seat. Xiaoning's name isn't on the roster at all.

Xiaoning pulls up the "regional regatta entry confirmation" the recruiter sent, saved on her phone. The date is the day she fell and was hospitalized—lying in a hospital bed, she couldn't have been on a boat.

Father and daughter finally confirm it: the so-called athletic recruit slot was the recruiter faking a record, Photoshopping a photo, and putting a person who never existed in her place.

Xiaoning saves the evidence and turns to her father.

XIAONING
The future you bought me—the person in it isn't even me.

### S06 Giving Up the Shortcut

The Father admits it: he couldn't face his daughter missing the rowing recruit showcase. He just wanted to grab an Ivy League slot.

Father and daughter save the recruiter's promises, the payment records, and the original materials. They proactively explain the situation to the schools involved, stop using the fake record, and offer to cooperate with any review.

The Father closes the loan page. He pushes the sketchbook back to her.

FATHER
Then let's start with what you can actually do.

### S07 Her Own Name

Some time later, Xiaoning applies to an art program with her own drawings. She gets a portfolio interview invitation.

She places the invitation and the self-portrait in front of her father.

XIAONING
This time, the name and the work—they're both mine.

For the first time, the Father reads through her yearbook, page by page.
