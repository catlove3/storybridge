# THE PEN EXCHANGE

## A 12-Scene Vertical Drama

---

### S01 — PAST LIFE: THE BORROWED PEN

**EXT. NATIONAL ACADEMIC CHAMPIONSHIP TESTING CENTER — MORNING**

*White banners flap against a gray sky: "NAC FINALS — DAY 4." Students stream toward glass doors, clutching clear plastic bags of pencils and calculators.*

LIN ZHIXIA, 18, walks with her head down, lips moving silently — reciting formulas. She's in a faded hoodie. Around her, kids in designer backpacks laugh too loudly.

SHEN MAN, 18, immaculate, falls into step beside her. Shen Man's smile is warm. Her eyes are not.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> Zhixia. Hey — my pen just died. Can I borrow one?

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> (barely looking up)
> Yeah, sure.

Lin Zhixia unzips her pencil case, pulls out a pen at random, hands it over.

Shen Man takes it. Tests it on her palm — two quick strokes. Hands it back.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> Thanks. This one's good.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> Mm.

Lin Zhixia pockets the pen without a thought. Turns toward the entrance.

CLOSE ON: Shen Man's fingers. As she returns the pen, her fingertip lingers on the barrel — half a second. A faint BLUE MARK glows on her palm, then fades.

Lin Zhixia doesn't see it.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

---

### S02 — PAST LIFE: SCORE NIGHT

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**

*Cheap apartment. A single desk lamp. Lin Zhixia sits before a laptop, fingers hovering.*

She types her ID number. Hits enter.

The screen loads. A number appears: **487.**

She blinks. Refreshes. 487. Refreshes again. 487.

Her hands begin to shake.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> (whisper)
> No. No, no, no —

Her MOTHER appears in the doorway, robe tied loose, eyes puffy from sleep.

<center>MOTHER</center>
> What is it? What's wrong?

Lin Zhixia can't speak. She turns the laptop. Her mother reads the number. Her legs give out. She sinks onto the edge of the bed.

<center>MOTHER</center>
> That's not... you said you had six-sixty. You said —

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> I know what I said.

Her phone rings. CALLER ID: MR. CHEN. She answers.

<center>MR. CHEN (V.O.)</center>
> Zhixia. I just checked the NAC portal. There has to be a mistake. I've coached for twenty years — I've never seen a drop like this. I'm filing a score review tonight.

**TIME CUT — THREE DAYS LATER**

Lin Zhixia stares at an email on her laptop. The subject line: *NAC Score Review — Determination.*

She reads aloud, voice flat:

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> "After thorough review, your score of 487 is confirmed. No discrepancy found."

**CUT TO: TV SCREEN — NAC NEWS COVERAGE**

Shen Man's face fills the frame. A chyron: *"NAC TOP SCORER: SHEN MAN, 669."*

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> Shen Man, daughter of Shen Group CEO Shen Weiguo, credits her success to "just doing my best."

Shen Man smiles for the camera. Then — for one second — her eyes go empty. She glances down at her hand. A faint BLUE MARK on her palm is fading.

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> Truly an inspiration — wealth and hard work, together.

**CLOSE ON: COMMENT SECTION scrolling.**

*"Rich AND smart? Life isn't fair."*
*"She earned it!"*
*"669 is insane. Congrats!"*

Lin Zhixia watches from her couch. Her face is stone.

---

### S03 — PAST LIFE: THE ROOFTOP

**EXT. HIGH SCHOOL ROOFTOP — DAY**

*Wind. Chain-link fence. The city sprawls below.*

Lin Zhixia stands at the edge. Her phone buzzes — an anonymous text: *"Come to the roof. You deserve the truth."*

Footsteps. SHEN MAN emerges from the stairwell door. No smile now. No warmth.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> You think I got 669 because of my father? His three million bought the review. But the swap — I did that myself.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> You... what?

Shen Man holds up her palm. The blue mark is almost invisible.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> That pen you lent me. It was perfect. You want to know why I had to borrow *yours*?

Lin Zhixia's blood runs cold. She turns toward the door.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> I'm calling the police.

She takes one step.

FOOTSTEPS RUSH BEHIND HER.

TWO HANDS SLAM INTO HER BACK.

**WIDE SHOT:** Lin Zhixia's body tips over the edge. The wind swallows her scream.

Shen Man stands at the railing, breathing hard. She leans over, looks down.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> (softly)
> Next life — don't lend your pen to anyone.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

---

### S04 — REBIRTH: THE SWITCH

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S BEDROOM — MORNING**

*Same room. Same desk lamp. But the light is different — early morning, golden.*

Lin Zhixia's eyes snap open. She sits up. Looks around.

Her NAC admission ticket is on the desk. Date: **Day 1 of Finals.**

Her mother's voice from the kitchen:

<center>MOTHER (O.S.)</center>
> Zhixia! Breakfast! You'll be late!

Lin Zhixia pinches the back of her hand. Hard. It hurts.

She exhales. Slowly.

She sits at her desk. Opens a notebook. Writes three lines:

*1. She borrows my pen to swap scores. The pen is the anchor.*
*2. Don't lend her MY pen.*
*3. The rooftop push — this time she hits air. And I record it.*

She closes the notebook. Checks her pencil case.

Her pen. A backup. And one more — a cheap blue pen with a cracked clip. ZHOU YU's pen. Left on her desk last week.

Lin Zhixia slides Zhou Yu's pen into the front pocket of the case.

**CLOSE ON: her face. Calm. Ready.**

---

### S05 — REBIRTH: THE TESTING CENTER

**EXT. NAC TESTING CENTER — MORNING**

*Same white banners. Same gray sky. Same students.*

Shen Man appears at Lin Zhixia's side. Same warm smile.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> Zhixia. My pen died. Can I borrow one?

Lin Zhixia smiles. Reaches into the front pocket. Pulls out Zhou Yu's blue pen.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> Here.

Shen Man takes it. Tests it on her palm — two strokes. Her grip is strange. Like she's feeling for something.

She hands it back. Her fingertip lingers on the barrel — half a second.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> Thanks.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> No problem.

Lin Zhixia takes the pen back. Drops it into her case. She doesn't look at it again.

**CLOSE ON: Shen Man's palm. A faint blue mark glows — then fades.**

---

### S06 — REPLAY: SCORE NIGHT

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**

*Same desk. Same lamp. Same laptop.*

Lin Zhixia types her ID. Hits enter.

**669.**

She stares. Refreshes. 669.

She exhales. A small, tight smile. Then it fades.

Her phone rings. CALLER ID: ZHOU YU. She answers.

<center>ZHOU YU (V.O.)</center>
> (voice shaking)
> Zhixia — I got 487. But I estimated 330. *330.* How is that possible?

Lin Zhixia grips the phone. Says nothing.

<center>ZHOU YU (V.O.)</center>
> Zhixia? Are you there?

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> I'm here.

She hangs up. Stares at the wall.

**CUT TO: CLASS GROUP CHAT — PHONE SCREEN**

Shen Man posts a screenshot. Her score: **330.**

Caption: *"How is this possible?"*

The chat goes silent. Then:

*"Man, is that a mistake?"*
*"Did you check the portal?"*

Shen Man doesn't reply.

---

### S07 — ZHOU YU'S SCORE

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S APARTMENT — DAY**

*Knock at the door. Lin Zhixia opens it.*

ZHOU YU, 18, eyes red, pushes past her into the room.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> I filed a score review. The NAC committee says my 487 is correct. But I *know* what I wrote. I estimated 330. *330.*

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> Zhou Yu —

The door slams open. SHEN MAN storms in. She grabs Zhou Yu's phone. Sees the 487.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> (snapping)
> You only had 330! How did you —

She stops. Freezes.

Zhou Yu stares at her.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> The scores just came out. How do *you* know what I had before?

Shen Man's face goes pale. She drops the phone. Turns. Walks out.

Silence.

Zhou Yu turns to Lin Zhixia.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> Zhixia. You know something.

Lin Zhixia doesn't answer.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> Tell me.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> (quiet)
> The pen. The one she borrowed. It was yours.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> What pen?

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> On the last day of finals. She asked to borrow a pen. I gave her yours.

Zhou Yu's face changes. Slowly.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> You did it on purpose.

Lin Zhixia doesn't deny it.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> So you protected yourself. And pushed me in front of the bus.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> I didn't think it would go this far. I thought she was swapping with —

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> You *thought*? That was my NAC score. My future. You *thought*?

Zhou Yu walks to the door. Opens it.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> Don't call me.

The door closes.

Lin Zhixia sits in the dark. She doesn't turn on the light.

---

### S08 — THE ROOFTOP: CLOSING THE NET

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*Phone rings. CALLER ID: SHEN MAN.*

Lin Zhixia answers.

<center>SHEN MAN (V.O.)</center>
> (trembling)
> It was you. You switched the pens.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> I didn't switch anything. I just didn't give you mine.

<center>SHEN MAN (V.O.)</center>
> Do you know how long I prepared for this?

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> Zhou Yu prepared longer.

Silence. Three seconds.

<center>SHEN MAN (V.O.)</center>
> You're dead.

**CUT TO: EXT. HIGH SCHOOL ROOFTOP — NEXT NIGHT**

*8:00 PM. Wind. The same chain-link fence.*

Lin Zhixia steps onto the roof. A small ACTION CAMERA is clipped to her backpack strap. A WIRE RECORDER is hidden in her sleeve.

ZHOU YU waits in the stairwell. Not to help — to hear Shen Man confess. To get her score back.

Shen Man emerges. She holds up a pen.

<center>SHEN MAN</center>
> Do you know what this is? If I write your name —

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> Write it.

Shen Man stops.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> You swapped Zhou Yu once. Swap me, and the system logs two entries. You can delete the server — you can't delete the offline backup. You have one move left: confess. Give Zhou Yu her score back.

Shen Man lunges.

Lin Zhixia sidesteps.

Shen Man's momentum carries her into the railing. She hits hard. Gasps.

FOOTSTEPS EXPLODE FROM THE STAIRWELL.

POLICE. ZHOU YU. And OLD SONG — 50s, tired eyes, a former NAC systems technician fired by Shen Group. He holds a stack of printed logs.

---

### S09 — THE LOGS

**INT. OLD SONG'S APARTMENT — THREE DAYS EARLIER**

*Cramped. Books everywhere. A single monitor glows.*

Lin Zhixia sits across from Old Song. He spreads printed pages on the table.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> Three days after score release, a post appeared on the NAC forums. Claimed to be from someone inside the scoring system. It was deleted in seconds. I saved the ID.

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> And you traced it.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> I used to work there. I know the architecture.

He taps a page.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> There's an unsigned module. Code name: "EASY-SWAP." Two fields. That's it.

He reads aloud:

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> "Borrower: Shen Man. Lent-by: Zhou Yu. Swap result: SUCCESS."

Lin Zhixia stares.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> Three days later, a new line appeared.

He points.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> "Target error. Recommend rollback."

<center>LIN ZHIXIA</center>
> The system knew it swapped the wrong person.

<center>OLD SONG</center>
> The system isn't stupid. It just doesn't care.

ZHOU YU stands in the doorway. She's been listening.

<center>ZHOU YU</center>
> (quiet)
> So my score was marked "recommend ignore"?

Old Song doesn't answer. He slides a USB drive across the table.

---

### S10 — THE TRIAL

**INT. COURTROOM — DAY**

*A fictional federal courtroom. Clean. Cold.*

Shen Man sits at the defense table. Her lawyer, SHARP IN A GRAY SUIT, addresses the judge.

<center>DEFENSE ATTORNEY</center>
> Your Honor, the prosecution's "system" is a fantasy. There is no evidence such a program exists.

The PROSECUTOR stands. Holds up three items.

<center>PROSECUTOR</center>
> Exhibit A: offline system logs recovered by a former NAC technician. Exhibit B: audio recording from the rooftop. Exhibit C: the defendant's own social media post from score night.

The prosecutor turns to Shen Man.

<center>PROSECUTOR</center>
> Ms. Shen. When you saw your score — 330 — your first call was to Lin Zhixia. Not the NAC committee. Not your father. Why?

Shen Man opens her mouth. Nothing comes out.

Lin Zhixia sits in the gallery. She doesn't look at her.

---

### S11 — THE RECKONING

**INT. NEWS BROADCAST — DAY**

*A chyron: "NAC SCORE-FRAUD SCANDAL: SHEN MAN ARRESTED."*

<center>ANCHOR (V.O.)</center>
> Shen Man has been arrested on charges of unauthorized access to a protected computer system and attempted murder. Her father, Shen Weiguo, is under investigation for bribing NAC review officials. Shen Group stocks have fallen for seven consecutive days. Banks have pulled credit lines. The company has filed for bankruptcy.

**CUT TO: LIN ZHIXIA'S APARTMENT — DAY**

*A letter arrives. Zhou Yu's restored NAC score report. 330 — her original score, reinstated.*

Lin Zhixia's phone buzzes. A message from Zhou Yu:

*"Score's back. But we can't go back."*

Lin Zhixia stares at the screen. Types one word:

*"Yeah."*

---

### S12 — EPILOGUE

**INT. LIN ZHIXIA'S APARTMENT — DAY**

*A thick envelope. University admissions. Lin Zhixia's mother wipes it three times before opening it.*

Lin Zhixia's phone buzzes. Zhou Yu:

*"I'm going to teach. So no one can swap a student's score again."*

Lin Zhixia smiles. Types back:

*"I'm going to law school."*

*"Why?"*

*"Because the system is still out there. Someone has to watch it."*

She takes Zhou Yu's pen — the blue one, cracked clip — and places it under the glass on her desk. Beside it, a note:

*"This time, every point is mine. Next time, no one takes it."*

She doesn't throw the pen away.

**CLOSE ON: the pen. Still. Waiting.**

**FADE TO BLACK.**

## ADAPTATION NOTE

This adaptation transposes the Chinese *gaokao* framework into the fictional **National Academic Championship (NAC)** — a national multi-day academic competition whose final scores determine elite university admissions and full scholarships. The NAC, its review committee, and all legal institutions depicted are entirely fictional. The "EASY-SWAP" system and its logs are preserved as the story's supernatural mechanism, with the pen remaining the anchor for borrower and lent-by identification. Score relationships are maintained exactly: Lin Zhixia 669 (unchanged), Zhou Yu 330→487, Shen Man 487→330. Shen Man's slip — "You only had 330!" — and Zhou Yu's confrontation with Lin Zhixia are preserved. The rooftop trap, Old Song's offline logs, the trial, score restoration, and the open-ended system epilogue all remain intact. Dialogue is kept short and shootable for vertical-drama pacing.
