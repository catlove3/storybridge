**THE GIRL WHO SWAPPED MY SCORE**

**A Rebirth Suspense Revenge Vertical Drama**

**American English Screenplay**

---

**S01 — PAST LIFE: THE PEN**

**EXT. MERIDIAN STATE ACADEMIC CHAMPIONSHIP — ARENA HALLWAY — DAY**

A massive convention center hallway. Banners read "MERIDIAN STATE ACADEMIC CHAMPIONSHIP — FINALS." Hundreds of high school students mill around in blazers and lanyards. Camera crews from the live broadcast weave through the crowd.

LYDIA CHEN, 17, sharp-eyed and focused, stands against a wall, silently reviewing flashcards. Her lips move without sound.

SERENA MONTGOMERY, 17, polished and poised, glides up beside her. Perfect smile. Designer backpack.

<center>SERENA</center>
> Lydia. Hey. My pen just died on me. Can I borrow one?

Lydia barely looks up. She pulls a pen from her pencil case and hands it over.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Sure.

Serena takes it. Tests it on a scrap of paper — two quick strokes. Hands it back.

<center>SERENA</center>
> Thanks. Nice pen.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Mm.

Lydia pockets the pen without a second thought. Turns back to her flashcards.

CLOSE ON: Serena's hand as she returns the pen. Her fingertip lingers on the barrel — half a second too long.

Lydia doesn't see it.

---

**S02 — PAST LIFE: RANKINGS NIGHT**

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

A laptop glows on the coffee table. Lydia sits cross-legged, refreshing the championship's live ranking page. Her mother, DIANE CHEN, hovers behind her.

The screen loads.

**RANKING: 487**

Lydia blinks. Refreshes. 487. Refreshes again. 487.

Her hands begin to tremble.

<center>DIANE</center>
> Sweetheart... what does it say?

Lydia can't speak. Diane leans in, reads the number, and sinks slowly onto the couch. Her face goes pale.

**INTERCUT — PHONE CALL**

COACH WELLS, 50s, Lydia's academic coach, his voice tight with disbelief.

<center>COACH WELLS (V.O.)</center>
> That's impossible. I've coached for twenty years. I'm filing an arbitration review right now.

**TIME CUT — THREE DAYS LATER**

Lydia stares at an official email on her laptop.

**ARBITRATION REVIEW: DENIED. FINAL SCORE CONFIRMED: 487.**

Four words. *Score confirmed. No error.*

**INTERCUT — LIVE BROADCAST — SERENA'S INTERVIEW**

On Lydia's laptop screen: Serena Montgomery, radiant under stage lights, a championship medal around her neck.

**RANKING: 940**

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> Serena, 940 — the highest score in Meridian Championship history. How does it feel?

<center>SERENA</center>
> I just... performed beyond myself, I guess.

She smiles. For one second, her eyes go empty. She glances down at her hand — the one that took the pen.

CLOSE ON: her palm. A faint blue mark, fading.

**CUT TO — SOCIAL MEDIA FEED**

Comments scroll:
*"Montgomery family AND a genius? Unfair."*
*"Old money + hard work = 940."*
*"She's literally perfect."*

Lydia watches from her dark bedroom. Her face is stone.

---

**S03 — PAST LIFE: THE ROOFTOP**

**EXT. ARENA CONVENTION CENTER — ROOFTOP — DUSK**

Wind. The city sprawls below. Lydia stands at the edge, phone in hand, reading an anonymous text:

**"Come alone. I'll tell you how it happened."**

The rooftop door opens. Serena steps out. No cameras. No smile. Her mask is gone.

<center>SERENA</center>
> You think I got here because of my father? His three million bought the arbitration review. But the swap? I did that myself.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You?

Serena holds up her palm. The blue mark is almost invisible now.

<center>SERENA</center>
> That pen of yours. It worked beautifully. You ever wonder why I *had* to borrow yours?

Lydia's blood runs cold. She turns to leave — to report, to call the police.

FOOTSTEPS behind her. Fast.

Two hands slam into her back.

She falls.

The wind screams past her ears. Serena's voice drifts down from the rooftop edge, calm as a lullaby:

<center>SERENA (V.O.)</center>
> Next time, don't lend your pen to just anyone.

The world goes black.

The official report will call it a suicide.

It was murder.

---

**S04 — REBIRTH: THE SWITCH**

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM — MORNING**

GASP. Lydia bolts upright in bed.

Sunlight. Her room. Her desk. Her mother's voice from downstairs:

<center>DIANE (O.S.)</center>
> Lydia! Breakfast! Championship Day One — you're not gonna be late!

Lydia grabs her phone. The date. Championship Day One. The exact morning from her memory.

She pinches the back of her hand. Hard. It hurts.

She sits frozen for ten minutes. Then opens a notebook and writes three lines:

*1. She borrows my pen to swap scores. The pen is the anchor.*
*2. Do NOT lend her my pen.*
*3. The rooftop push — this time she hits air. And it gets recorded.*

She closes the notebook. Checks her pencil case: her pen. A backup. And one more — a pen ZOE RAMIREZ left on her desk last week.

She slides Zoe's pen into the outermost pocket of her pencil case.

---

**S05 — REBIRTH: THE ARENA**

**INT. ARENA — FINAL ROUND CHECK-IN — DAY**

The last round. Individual written section. Students at tables, proctors pacing. Live cameras on overhead rails.

Serena appears beside Lydia. Same smile. Same script.

<center>SERENA</center>
> Lydia. My pen just died. Can I borrow one?

Lydia smiles. Pulls the pen from the outermost pocket.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Here.

Serena takes it. Tests it — two strokes. Lydia watches her grip. Strange. Like she's feeling for something inside the pen.

Serena hands it back. Her fingertip lingers on the barrel — half a second.

Exactly like before.

Lydia takes it back. Drops it into her pencil case. Never touches it again.

---

**S06 — REPLAY: RANKINGS**

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

Laptop. Live ranking page. Diane behind her.

Lydia types her ID. Hits enter.

**RANKING: 940**

She stares. Refreshes. 940. Exactly what she estimated.

Her phone rings. ZOE RAMIREZ, 17, breathless.

<center>ZOE (V.O.)</center>
> Lydia. I got 487. But I estimated like 330. What the hell?

Lydia grips the phone. A slow smile spreads across her face. Then stops halfway.

**INTERCUT — CLASS GROUP CHAT**

Messages explode. Serena has posted a screenshot:

**RANKING: 330**

Caption: *"330. How is this possible?"*

Silence. Then, timidly:

*"Serena... is the page glitching?"*

No reply from Serena.

---

**S07 — ZOE'S SCORE**

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME — KITCHEN — DAY**

ZOE bursts through the door, phone held up like evidence.

<center>ZOE</center>
> Arbitration review came back. 487. Confirmed. But I *know* I only scored 330.

Before Lydia can speak — the front door shoves open.

SERENA storms in. She snatches the phone from Zoe's hand. Sees 487.

<center>SERENA</center>
> You only had 330!

Silence.

Zoe stares at her.

<center>ZOE</center>
> Scores just came out. How do you know what I had before?

The room freezes. Serena's face twitches. She slams the phone onto the counter and turns to leave.

Zoe steps in front of the door. Shuts it.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She knows my original score.

Lydia reaches into her pencil case. Pulls out the pen.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> She borrowed a pen before the final round. I gave her yours.

Zoe looks at the pen. Looks at Serena's 330 on the group chat. It clicks.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She tried to steal your 940... and gave me her 487 instead?

Lydia nods.

Zoe pockets her phone.

<center>ZOE</center>
> I'm keeping this score. Her debt — we settle together.

---

**S08 — ALLIANCE: THE TRAP**

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME — BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Lydia's phone buzzes. Serena. Voice shaking with rage.

<center>SERENA (V.O.)</center>
> It was you. That pen you gave me wasn't yours.

Lydia hits record.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> I never said it was. You borrowed it. You did the swap.

<center>SERENA (V.O.)</center>
> Give me back the 487.

Zoe, sitting nearby, silently points at the ceiling. Mouths: *Rooftop.*

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You want it back? Tomorrow. 8 PM. The old place. Tell me face-to-face how the swap works.

Beat.

<center>SERENA (V.O.)</center>
> Come, and I'll make sure you don't leave alive.

Click.

Zoe mounts a second phone on a shelf. A live-stream test screen blinks green.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She wants a rooftop? Let's give her one the whole school can watch.

---

**S09 — ROOFTOP: LIVE**

**EXT. ARENA CONVENTION CENTER — ROOFTOP — NIGHT**

8 PM. Wind. Lydia stands alone in the center. A small action camera clipped to her backpack strap points at the door.

Zoe hides in the stairwell, phone streaming live.

The door opens. Serena steps out. The blue mark on her palm flickers — bright, dim, bright.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> My 940 didn't move. Zoe got 487. You dropped to 330. Why?

Serena's mask cracks.

<center>SERENA</center>
> Because the system only recognizes the pen's *original owner*. The borrower's score swaps with whoever owns the pen. I was supposed to swap with *you*. How was I supposed to know that pen belonged to Zoe?

<center>LYDIA</center>
> So you wanted my 940.

<center>SERENA</center>
> Yes. But you made me swap with the wrong person. Once scores are published, the swap locks. Nobody can undo it. Zoe walks around with my 487 — does she deserve it?

**INTERCUT — LIVE STREAM CHAT**

Viewer count spirals. 3,000. 5,000. 10,000.

*"Is this real??"*
*"She just confessed to everything"*
*"SOMEONE CALL THE POLICE"*

Serena doesn't notice. She steps toward Lydia.

<center>SERENA</center>
> You ruined me. So you're not walking off this roof.

---

**S10 — REPLAY: THE FALL THAT NEVER HAPPENS**

Serena lunges. Same angle. Same force as before.

Lydia sidesteps.

Serena hits air. Stumbles. Slams into the railing.

The rooftop door bursts open. Zoe walks out, phone held high, still streaming.

<center>ZOE</center>
> Keep going. Ten thousand people just heard you. Who you tried to rob. Who you tried to push.

Serena goes white. She lunges for the phone.

SIRENS. Stairwell. Security and police flood the rooftop. They pin Serena down.

<center>SERENA</center>
> The 487 was MINE! They set me up!

Lydia looks at her.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You borrowed the pen. You did the swap. You threw the punch.

**TIME CUT — MONTHS LATER — COURTROOM**

**INT. SUPERIOR COURT OF MERIDIAN COUNTY — DAY**

Clean. Modern. A judge, 60s, stern.

PROSECUTOR presents: the recorded death threat from Serena's phone call. The rooftop livestream. The action camera footage. The scene report.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> This court is not here to litigate past lives. Nor does it need to prove whether any so-called "system" exists. What this court is here to determine is whether you, Serena Montgomery, made a death threat and committed an act of attempted murder on that rooftop.

He holds up a printed screenshot.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> But this explains your motive. The moment scores were published — how did you know Zoe Ramirez originally scored 330? And why did you insist her 487 belonged to you?

Serena opens her mouth. Nothing comes out.

---

**S11 — RECKONING**

**INT. COURTROOM — CONTINUOUS**

The judge reads the verdict.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> On the charge of attempted murder in the second degree, based on the death threat, the livestream evidence, the recorded footage, and the physical act — this court finds you guilty.

**CUT TO — NEWS MONTAGE**

Education officials at a press conference.

<center>OFFICIAL (V.O.)</center>
> The Meridian State Academic Championship has completed its review. No evidence of traditional cheating or manual tampering was found. Official scores stand: Lydia Chen, 940. Zoe Ramirez, 487. Serena Montgomery, 330.

**CUT TO — SERENA'S HOME — WEEKS LATER**

A rejection letter from the state university. Her admission revoked. Her scholarship forfeited. A school disciplinary notice.

**CUT TO — ZOE'S APARTMENT — DAY**

Zoe photographs her acceptance letter. Texts Lydia:

*"487. Confirmed. Full ride. She tried to steal your 940 and handed me her score instead."*

Lydia replies:

*"She finally solved one problem correctly."*

---

**S12 — EPILOGUE**

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME — LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Lydia's mother wipes the acceptance envelope three times before opening it.

The doorbell rings. Zoe stands outside, holding her own acceptance letter in one hand and two boba teas in the other.

<center>ZOE</center>
> One 940. One 487. Both delivered.

Lydia takes a tea. Smiles.

Zoe sets the pen on the table between the two letters.

<center>ZOE</center>
> What do we do with this?

Lydia picks it up. Slides it under the glass top of her desk.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Keep it. Not as a souvenir of the system. As a souvenir of her greed.

They walk out of the room.

**CLOSE ON: THE PEN.**

Under the glass. Still. Silent.

Beat.

A faint blue glow pulses deep inside the barrel.

Then fades.

**FADE TO BLACK.**
