# SHE SWAPPED MY EXAM SCORE

**A Vertical Drama in 12 Episodes**

---

## S01 — THE PEN

**EXT. EXAM HALL - MORNING**

*Fifteen minutes before the final exam. Students mill outside a massive testing center, clutching review sheets and water bottles. The air hums with nervous energy.*

**LYDIA CHEN**, 18, sharp-eyed and composed, stands apart, silently reciting essay outlines. She's the kind of student who's been preparing for this day her entire life.

**MIA PRESCOTT**, 18, designer backpack, perfectly styled hair, approaches with a practiced smile. She's the girl whose father donated a new library wing.

<center>MIA</center>
> Hey, Lydia. My pen just died on me. Can I borrow one?

Lydia barely glances up, pulls a pen from her pencil case, hands it over.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Sure.

Mia takes it, scribbles on a scrap of paper. Tests the ink. Her grip is strange—deliberate, almost ceremonial.

<center>MIA</center>
> *(returning it)*
> Thanks. This one's nice.

Lydia pockets the pen without looking. Doesn't notice Mia's fingertip lingering on the barrel for half a second longer than necessary.

**CLOSE ON:** Mia's palm as she pulls her hand back. A faint blue mark, like ink bleeding under the skin, pulses once. Then fades.

---

## S02 — THE SCORE

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM - NIGHT**

*Midnight. Lydia sits before her laptop, fingers hovering over the keyboard. Her mother, **GRACE CHEN**, watches from the doorway.*

Lydia types her ID number. Hits enter.

**SCREEN: 487.**

She blinks. Refreshes. 487. Refreshes again. 487.

Her hands begin to shake.

<center>GRACE</center>
> *(stepping closer)*
> What is it? Let me see—

She sees the screen. Her legs give out. She sinks onto the edge of the bed.

<center>GRACE</center>
> That's... that's not possible. You were predicted 670. You said the test felt easy.

Lydia doesn't answer. She's already dialing.

**INTERCUT - PHONE CALL**

**MR. HARRINGTON**, 50s, Lydia's AP English teacher, sounds like he's been punched.

<center>HARRINGTON (V.O.)</center>
> I've been teaching for twenty years. I've never seen a discrepancy like this. I'll file for a score verification first thing tomorrow.

**THREE DAYS LATER**

Lydia stares at an official letter. Four words: **"Score verified. No errors."**

**INTERCUT - TV SCREEN**

A local news segment. Mia Prescott, radiant in the spotlight, accepts congratulations.

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> ...with an unprecedented 669, Mia Prescott tops the district. Mia, what's your secret?

<center>MIA</center>
> *(on TV)*
> I just... performed beyond my expectations.

For a split second, her eyes go empty. She glances down at her hand—the one that took the pen. A faint blue mark on her palm is fading.

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> And from the Prescott family, a reminder that hard work and privilege can go hand in hand!

Social media comments scroll: *"Rich AND smart? Goals." "She earned it!" "Queen."*

Lydia watches. Her jaw tightens.

---

## S03 — THE ROOFTOP

**EXT. SCHOOL ROOFTOP - DUSK**

*The day college applications are due. Lydia climbs the stairs to the rooftop, summoned by an anonymous text. Wind whips her hair.*

Mia stands at the edge, back to the city skyline. No audience. No cameras. The mask is off.

<center>MIA</center>
> You think I got here because of my father? His three million bought the verification. But the swap—I did that myself.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You?

Mia holds up her palm. The blue mark is barely visible now, a ghost of a stain.

<center>MIA</center>
> That pen of yours. It worked beautifully. You want to know why I had to borrow *yours*?

Lydia's blood runs cold. She turns to leave—to report this, to call the police, to do *something*.

Footsteps behind her. Fast.

Two hands slam into her back.

**THE FALL.**

Wind roars past her ears. Above, Mia's voice drifts down from the rooftop edge, calm as a lullaby:

<center>MIA (V.O.)</center>
> Next time, don't lend your pen to just anyone.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

The world will call it a suicide. It was murder from start to finish.

---

## S04 — RESET

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM - DAWN**

*Gasp. Lydia's eyes fly open.*

She's in her bed. Her room. The morning light is wrong—too golden, too early.

She sits up. On her desk: her exam admission ticket. Date: **June 7th**. The first day of exams.

Her mother's voice from downstairs:

<center>GRACE (O.S.)</center>
> Lydia! Breakfast! You don't want to be late!

Lydia pinches the back of her hand. Hard. It hurts.

She sits frozen for ten minutes. Then she opens a notebook and writes three lines:

> **1. She borrows the pen to swap scores. The pen is the anchor.**
> **2. Do NOT lend her my pen.**
> **3. The rooftop push—this time she hits air. And I record everything.**

She checks her pencil case. Her own pens. A spare. And one more—a pen **ZOE WALSH** left on her desk last week.

Lydia slides Zoe's pen into the outermost pocket of her case.

---

## S05 — THE EXAM

**EXT. EXAM HALL - MORNING**

*Same morning. Same crowd. Same nervous energy.*

Lydia stands in the same spot, reciting the same essay outlines. But her eyes are different—older, harder.

Mia approaches. Same practiced smile.

<center>MIA</center>
> Hey, Lydia. My pen just died. Can I borrow one?

Lydia smiles. It doesn't reach her eyes.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Sure.

She pulls the pen from the outermost pocket. Zoe's pen. Hands it over.

Mia takes it. Scribbles. Her grip is strange again—feeling for something.

Lydia watches closely. Mia's fingertip lingers on the barrel for half a second.

Then she hands it back.

<center>MIA</center>
> Thanks.

Lydia takes it, drops it into her case, and doesn't touch it again.

---

## S06 — THE SCORE, REPRISE

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM - NIGHT**

*Midnight. Same laptop. Same trembling fingers.*

Lydia types her ID. Hits enter.

**SCREEN: 669.**

She stares. Refreshes. 669. Her predicted score. Exactly.

She exhales—a breath she's been holding for a lifetime.

Her phone rings. **ZOE WALSH**, her lab partner, voice shaking.

<center>ZOE (V.O.)</center>
> Lydia... I got 487. But I estimated 330. *330.* How is that possible?

Lydia grips the phone. A slow smile spreads across her face. Then stops halfway.

**INTERCUT - CLASS GROUP CHAT**

Mia Prescott has posted a screenshot. Her score: **330.**

Caption: *"How? This can't be right."*

Silence. Then, cautiously:

<center>CLASSMATE (V.O.)</center>
> Mia... did you check the number right?

No response. Mia goes offline.

---

## S07 — ZOE'S SCORE

**INT. LYDIA'S LIVING ROOM - NEXT DAY**

*Zoe bursts through the door, phone held aloft like a torch.*

<center>ZOE</center>
> Verification came back. 487. Confirmed. But I *know* I only got 330. I counted. I *know.*

Before Lydia can respond, the front door slams open.

Mia storms in. She snatches the phone from Zoe's hand. Sees the 487.

<center>MIA</center>
> *(without thinking)*
> You only got 330! How did you—

She freezes.

Zoe stares at her. The room goes dead silent.

<center>ZOE</center>
> The scores just came out. How do *you* know what I got?

Mia's face drains of color. She drops the phone, turns, and walks out. Zoe slams the door behind her.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She knew my original score. Before anyone else.

Lydia reaches into her bag. Pulls out the pen.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> She borrowed a pen before the last exam. I gave her yours.

Zoe looks at the pen. Then at Mia's 330 in the group chat. Then back at Lydia.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She tried to steal your 669... and gave me her 487 instead?

Lydia nods.

Zoe pockets her phone. Her jaw sets.

<center>ZOE</center>
> I'm keeping this score. And her debt? We settle it together.

---

## S08 — THE TRAP

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM - THAT NIGHT**

*Lydia's phone buzzes. Mia. Voice shaking with rage.*

<center>MIA (V.O.)</center>
> It was you. The pen you gave me wasn't yours.

Lydia hits record. Keeps her voice level.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> I never said it was. You borrowed it. You did the swap.

<center>MIA (V.O.)</center>
> Give me back the 487.

Zoe, sitting beside Lydia, silently points upward. *The rooftop.*

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You want it back? Tomorrow. 8 PM. The old place. Tell me face to face how the swap works.

A pause. Then:

<center>MIA (V.O.)</center>
> Come if you dare. I'll make sure you don't leave this time.

Click. Dial tone.

Zoe is already setting up a second phone on a shelf, testing a live stream. A green light blinks on.

<center>ZOE</center>
> She wants a rooftop? Let's give her one the whole school can watch.

---

## S09 — THE ROOFTOP, LIVE

**EXT. SCHOOL ROOFTOP - NEXT NIGHT**

*8 PM. Lydia stands alone in the center of the rooftop, backpack strap holding a small action camera aimed at the door.*

Zoe hides in the stairwell, phone streaming live.

Mia pushes through the door. Her palm—the blue mark—flickers like a dying bulb.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> My 669 didn't move. Zoe got 487. You got 330. Why?

Mia's composure cracks. The truth spills out.

<center>MIA</center>
> Because the system only recognizes the pen's *original owner*! The borrower's score swaps with whoever the pen belongs to. I was supposed to swap with *you*. How was I supposed to know that pen was Zoe's?

<center>LYDIA</center>
> So you wanted my 669.

<center>MIA</center>
> *(cold laugh)*
> Yes. But you made me swap with the wrong person. Once scores are published, the exchange locks. No one can undo it. Zoe has my 487? She doesn't deserve it.

**INTERCUT - STAIRWELL**

Zoe watches the viewer count climb. 3,000. 5,000. 10,000.

Mia doesn't notice. She steps toward Lydia.

<center>MIA</center>
> You ruined me. So I'll make sure you never leave this rooftop.

---

## S10 — THE PUSH THAT MISSES

**EXT. SCHOOL ROOFTOP - CONTINUOUS**

*Mia lunges. Same angle. Same force as before.*

But Lydia is ready. She sidesteps.

Mia stumbles, crashes into the railing.

The rooftop door bursts open. Zoe steps out, phone held high, live stream still running.

<center>ZOE</center>
> Keep going. Ten thousand people just heard everything—whose score you tried to steal, and who you tried to push.

Mia's face goes white. She lunges for the phone.

Sirens. Police and security flood the rooftop. They pin Mia to the ground.

<center>MIA</center>
> *(screaming)*
> The 487 was mine! They set me up!

Lydia looks down at her.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You borrowed the pen. You did the swap. You pushed first.

**MONTHS LATER - COURTROOM**

The prosecutor presents: the death threat from Mia's phone call. The rooftop livestream. The action camera footage. The crime scene report.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> This court does not adjudicate past lives. Nor does it need to prove whether any "system" exists. What we are here to determine is that you made a death threat and attempted to push someone off a roof.

He holds up the screenshot of Mia's 330.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> But this explains your motive. The scores had just been released. How did you know Zoe's original score was 330? And why did you insist her 487 belonged to you?

Mia opens her mouth. Nothing comes out.

---

## S11 — THE RECKONING

**INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS**

The court finds Mia Prescott guilty of attempted murder.

As for the score swap—the Department of Education can only review the tests and system records. No traditional cheating. No manual tampering. The official scores stand: **Lydia 669. Zoe 487. Mia 330.**

The supernatural exchange cannot be used as evidence. But no real-world mechanism can reverse it either.

Mia's college admissions are revoked due to the criminal case and school disciplinary action.

**INT. LYDIA'S BEDROOM - DAY**

An acceptance letter arrives. Zoe sends a photo of her own letter.

<center>ZOE (TEXT)</center>
> 487. Confirmed. She tried to steal your 669 and gave me her score instead.

<center>LYDIA (TEXT)</center>
> This time she finally solved one problem correctly.

---

## S12 — EPILOGUE

**INT. LYDIA'S HOME - DAY**

*The acceptance letter arrives. Grace wipes the envelope three times before opening it.*

The doorbell rings. Zoe stands outside, holding her own letter and two bubble teas.

<center>ZOE</center>
> One 669. One 487. Both delivered.

Lydia laughs, takes a tea.

Zoe sets the pen between the two letters.

<center>ZOE</center>
> What about this?

Lydia slides it under the glass top of her desk.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Keep it. Not as a souvenir of the system—but as a reminder that she lost to her own greed.

They walk out together.

**CLOSE ON:** The pen under the glass.

Silence.

Then—deep inside the barrel—a faint blue light flickers.

And dies.

**FADE TO BLACK.**
