## 最终提示词

Use case: ads-marketing
Create one polished Chinese product competition poster for StoryBridge. Portrait A3 proportions, 1:1.414 aspect ratio, very high resolution, full bleed, ample margins. A coherent restrained editorial design, not a technology template. Ivory paper background #FAF9F5, deep navy #152D3C, teal #267868, a little muted gold #B79456. Large clean contemporary Chinese typography, meticulous hierarchy and generous negative space. No provider badge, no AI watermark, no decorative text, no background music symbols, no QR code, no claims about accuracy or user counts.

Product: an assistant for short drama scriptwriters adapting a Chinese story for a different culture. Key idea is dependency-aware rewriting: change a cultural premise and see which related scenes must change too, while preserving the emotional plot.

Reading order:
Top 25%: StoryBridge brand, short product descriptor, then large two-line headline.
Middle 45%: one simple elegant conceptual illustration: three screenplay pages joined by a delicate, clearly visible branching line. A Chinese examination sheet on the first page transforms into a rowing team roster on the second; on the final page a girl's sketchbook remains unchanged. Small ink drawings only, no people, no UI mockups, no giant globe, no complex network. The image shows the logic: setting changes → clue changes → emotional ending stays. Use small clear labels directly under the three pages.
Bottom 30%: one problem statement, three short workflow labels in a row, audience line. Comfortable whitespace, avoid card-heavy layouts.

All poster text must be exactly this Chinese and English content, no other text:
"StoryBridge"
"短剧出海的跨文化改编助手"
"让故事换个文化，"
"依然讲得通。"
Labels below the three illustrated pages:
"换设定" / "改线索" / "留情感"
Problem line:
"一处设定变了，哪些戏必须一起改？"
Workflow:
"选改法  →  看牵连  →  审改稿"
Audience at the bottom:
"面向短剧编剧与出海改编团队"

Keep the product name and product idea prominent and instantly intelligible. Flat finished poster only, not a photographed print, not a mockup on a wall.
