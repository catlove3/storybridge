**S01 — THE BORROWED PEN**

**EXT. WESTBROOK ACADEMY — CERTIFICATION EXAM HALL — MORNING**

A sprawling campus of red brick and glass. Students stream toward the exam hall, clutching IDs and clear plastic pouches.

LYDIA CHEN, 17, sharp-eyed, stands apart, mouthing vocabulary from a dog-eared index card. Her pen pouch is open. Three pens inside.

MADELINE SHAW, 17, polished and poised, glides up. She smiles — warm, practiced.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> Lydia. My pen just died. Can I borrow one?

Lydia doesn't look up. Pulls a pen from the pouch. Hands it over.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Yeah. Here.

Madeline takes it. Tests it on her palm — two quick strokes. Hands it back.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> Thanks. Good pen.

Lydia shrugs. Slips it back into the pouch. Doesn't notice:

Madeline's fingertip lingers on the barrel. Half a second. A faint blue shimmer pulses under her skin — then fades.

**S02 — THE NIGHT THE NUMBERS CAME**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

Lydia sits at a laptop. Her mother, DIANE, hovers behind her.

The screen loads. A number appears:

**487**

Lydia blinks. Refreshes. 487. Refreshes. 487.

Her hands start to shake.

<center>DIANE</center>
> What does it say?

Lydia can't speak. Diane leans in. Sees the number. Her knees buckle. She grabs the chair.

**INTERCUT — PHONE CALL**

MR. HARRIS, 50s, Lydia's AP Statistics teacher, on the line.

<center>MR. HARRIS (V.O.)</center>
> That's not possible. I've been teaching for twenty years. I'll file a data audit request first thing.

**TIME CUT — THREE DAYS LATER**

A letter on the kitchen table. Lydia reads it aloud, voice flat.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> "Audit complete. Score verified. No discrepancy found."

Diane covers her mouth.

**INTERCUT — TV SCREEN**

A local news segment. Madeline Shaw, radiant, stands beside her father, VICTOR SHAW, a tech billionaire.

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> Madeline Shaw tops the APC index with a 669 — the highest in Westbrook history. Madeline, what's your secret?

<center>MADELINE</center>
> I just... performed above expectations.

A beat. Her eyes go empty. She glances down at her hand — the one that took the pen. A faint blue line on her palm. Fading.

<center>REPORTER (V.O.)</center>
> And with a father like Victor Shaw, the sky's the limit!

**S03 — THE ROOF**

**EXT. WESTBROOK ACADEMY — ROOFTOP — DAY**

Wind. Gray sky. Lydia stands at the edge. Behind her, the door opens.

Madeline steps out. No smile now. No cameras.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> You think I got here because of my father? His three million bought the audit. The swap — I did that myself.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You?

Madeline holds up her palm. The blue line is almost gone.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> That pen of yours. It worked beautifully. You ever wonder why I had to borrow *yours*?

Lydia turns to leave. To report. To call someone.

Footsteps behind her. Fast.

Two hands slam into her back.

She falls.

The wind roars. Madeline's voice drifts down from the edge:

<center>MADELINE</center>
> Next time, don't lend your pen to anyone.

The world calls it a suicide. It was a murder.

**S04 — RESET**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LYDIA'S BEDROOM — DAWN**

Lydia's eyes snap open.

She sits up. Looks around. Her room. Her desk. Her mother's voice downstairs.

<center>DIANE (O.S.)</center>
> Lydia! Breakfast! You'll be late!

Lydia grabs her phone. The date: the first day of certification exams. The same day as before.

She pinches the back of her hand. Hard. It hurts.

She sits still for ten minutes. Then opens a notebook. Writes three lines:

*1. She borrows the pen to swap scores. The pen is the anchor.*
*2. Don't lend her my pen.*
*3. The roof — this time she pushes air. And I record it.*

She closes the notebook. Checks her pen pouch. Her pens. Spares. And one more — a pen ZOE REYES left on her desk last week.

She slides Zoe's pen into the outermost pocket.

**S05 — THE EXAM**

**EXT. WESTBROOK ACADEMY — CERTIFICATION EXAM HALL — MORNING**

Same campus. Same students. Same tension.

Madeline glides up. Same smile.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> Lydia. My pen just died. Can I borrow one?

Lydia smiles. Pulls the pen from the outermost pocket.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Sure. Here.

Madeline takes it. Tests it on her palm. Her grip is strange — like she's feeling for something.

She hands it back. Her fingertip lingers on the barrel. Half a second.

Lydia takes it. Drops it into her pouch. Doesn't touch it again.

**S06 — THE NUMBERS, AGAIN**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

Lydia at the laptop. Diane behind her.

The screen loads.

**669**

Lydia stares. Refreshes. 669.

Her phone rings. ZOE REYES, 17, breathless.

<center>ZOE (V.O.)</center>
> Lydia — I got 487. But I estimated 330. How is that possible?

Lydia grips the phone. A slow smile spreads. Then stops.

**INTERCUT — SOCIAL MEDIA**

A post from Madeline Shaw. A screenshot of her API: **330.**

Caption: *"How is this possible?"*

Comments flood in. Then go quiet. Someone finally writes:

*"Madeline... did you check the system?"*

No reply.

**S07 — ZOE'S SCORE**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Zoe bursts through the door. Eyes red.

<center>ZOE</center>
> I filed a data audit. They said my 487 is correct. But I estimated three hundred and thirty!

Lydia opens her mouth. The door slams open.

Madeline storms in. Grabs Zoe's phone. Sees 487.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> You only had 330!

Zoe freezes.

<center>ZOE</center>
> The scores just came out. How do you know what I had before?

Madeline's face goes pale. She drops the phone. Turns. Walks out.

Zoe looks at Lydia.

<center>ZOE</center>
> Lydia. Do you know something?

Long silence.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> The pen. The one she borrowed. It was yours.

<center>ZOE</center>
> What pen?

<center>LYDIA</center>
> The certification exam. She asked to borrow a pen. I gave her yours.

Zoe's face changes. Slowly.

<center>ZOE</center>
> You did it on purpose?

Lydia doesn't deny it.

<center>ZOE</center>
> So you protected yourself. And pushed me in front of the bus?

<center>LYDIA</center>
> I didn't think it would—

<center>ZOE</center>
> You didn't think? That was my future. My *life*. You didn't think?

The door slams. Lydia sits in the dark. Doesn't turn on the light.

**S08 — THE ROOF, PART TWO**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LYDIA'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Lydia's phone rings. Madeline. Voice shaking.

<center>MADELINE (V.O.)</center>
> It was you. You switched the pen.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> I didn't switch anything. I just didn't give you mine.

<center>MADELINE (V.O.)</center>
> Do you know how long I prepared for this?

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Zoe prepared longer.

Silence. Three seconds.

<center>MADELINE (V.O.)</center>
> You're dead.

**EXT. WESTBROOK ACADEMY — ROOFTOP — NEXT NIGHT**

8 PM. Lydia arrives. A GoPro clipped to her backpack strap. A recorder in her sleeve.

Zoe waits in the stairwell. Not to help — to hear Madeline confess. To get her score back.

Madeline steps onto the roof. Holding a pen.

<center>MADELINE</center>
> You know what this is? All I have to do is write your name—

<center>LYDIA</center>
> Write it.

Madeline stops.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You swapped Zoe once. Swap me, that's two entries in the log. The server can be wiped. Offline backups can't. You have one move left: confess. Give Zoe her score back.

Madeline lunges.

Lydia sidesteps. Madeline stumbles. Hits the railing.

Footsteps thunder up the stairs. Police. Zoe. And ARTHUR SLOAN, 60s, former data engineer for the National Education Data Alliance — holding a stack of printed logs.

**S09 — THE LOG**

**INT. ARTHUR SLOAN'S APARTMENT — THREE DAYS EARLIER**

Lydia knocks. Arthur opens. Wary.

<center>LYDIA</center>
> You worked on the APC system. You posted about it once. The post was deleted. I remembered your username.

Arthur stares. Then steps aside.

**INT. ARTHUR'S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS**

Papers spread across a table. Arthur points to a module.

<center>ARTHUR</center>
> An unsigned anomaly. Code name: EduSwap. Two fields. "Borrower: Madeline Shaw. Source: Zoe Reyes. Swap result: success."

<center>LYDIA</center>
> And later?

Arthur flips a page. A new line:

*"Target error. Recommend rollback."*

<center>LYDIA</center>
> The system knew it swapped the wrong person.

<center>ARTHUR</center>
> The system isn't stupid. It just doesn't care.

Zoe stands in the doorway. Voice barely audible.

<center>ZOE</center>
> So my score was... "recommended for deletion"?

Arthur says nothing. Slides a USB drive across the table.

**S10 — THE HEARING**

**INT. DISTRICT COURT — DAY**

A courtroom. Madeline at the defense table. Her lawyer, sharp-suited.

<center>DEFENSE ATTORNEY</center>
> There is no evidence this "EduSwap" exists. It's a fantasy.

The PROSECUTOR stands. Holds up three items.

<center>PROSECUTOR</center>
> Exhibit A: offline logs from the National Education Data Alliance, provided by a former engineer. Exhibit B: audio recording from the rooftop. Exhibit C: the defendant's own social media post — timestamped the night scores were released.

The JUDGE turns to Madeline.

<center>JUDGE</center>
> Ms. Shaw. When you saw your score was 487, why did you call Lydia Chen instead of filing an audit request?

Madeline opens her mouth. Nothing comes out.

Lydia sits in the gallery. Doesn't look at her.

**S11 — THE RECKONING**

**INT. NEWS STUDIO — DAY**

A news anchor reads from a teleprompter.

<center>ANCHOR (V.O.)</center>
> Madeline Shaw has been arrested on charges of unauthorized data manipulation and attempted murder. Her father, Victor Shaw, is under investigation for bribing Alliance officials to suppress audit requests. Shaw Technologies has lost seventy percent of its value. Banks have called in their loans. The company has filed for bankruptcy.

**INTERCUT — ZOE'S APARTMENT**

Zoe opens an envelope. Her official API: **330** — restored to her original score.

She texts Lydia:

*"Score's back. But we're not."*

Lydia reads it. Stares at the screen. Types one word:

*"Yeah."*

**S12 — CODA**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Lydia's college acceptance packet arrives. Diane wipes the envelope three times before opening it.

Lydia's phone buzzes. Zoe:

*"I'm going to teach. No one's swapping my students' scores."*

Lydia smiles. Types back:

*"I'm going to law school."*

*"Why?"*

*"Because the system's still out there. Someone has to watch it."*

She takes the pen — Zoe's pen — and places it under the glass on her desk. Beside it, a sticky note:

*"This time, every point was mine. Next time, no one takes them."*

But she doesn't throw the pen away.

The system didn't disappear. It just changed shape. And it's waiting for the next person who lends a pen.

**FADE OUT.**
