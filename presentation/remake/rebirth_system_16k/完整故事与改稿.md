## 中文 demo 原稿

《她换走了我的高考》（重生悬疑 · 12 场）

【S01 前世·借笔】
最后一科开考前，考场外。林知夏默背着作文素材，沈曼迎上来："知夏，笔借我用一下，我那支突然断墨了。"林知夏随手从笔袋里抽出一支递给她。沈曼接过去，在草稿纸上画了两下，还回来："谢啦，你这笔挺好用的。"林知夏没在意。她没看见，沈曼还笔时，指尖在笔杆上停了半秒。

【S02 前世·出分之夜】
深夜查分，屏幕跳出"487"。林知夏刷新了十几次，手越来越抖，母亲凑过来看了一眼，腿一软坐在椅子上。班主任老陈在电话里也懵了："不可能，我教了二十年书……我马上帮你申请复核。"三天后复核驳回，答复只有四个字：成绩无误。与此同时，沈曼以"669分"登上全校榜首，采访里说"我只是超常发挥"——说完眼神空了一秒，低头看了看自己接笔的那只手：掌心一道淡蓝色的痕，正在褪色。评论区都在夸"豪门还这么努力"。

【S03 前世·天台】
志愿填报截止那天，匿名短信把她约上天台。沈曼卸下所有伪装："你以为我是靠我爸？他那三百万，是买复核的。换分——是我自己换的。"林知夏："你？"沈曼伸出手，掌心的蓝痕已经淡得看不见了："你那支笔，挺好用的。你猜，为什么我非要借你的笔？"林知夏转身要走——去举报，去报警。身后脚步声逼近，两只手猛地推上她的背。坠落的风声里，沈曼的声音从天台边缘飘下来："下辈子，别随便把笔借人。"世人以为的自杀，从头到尾是一场谋杀。

【S04 重生·换牌】
再睁眼，是高考第一天清晨。桌上的准考证、门口母亲的粥、手机上的日期——和记忆分毫不差。林知夏掐了把手背，疼的。她坐在桌前缓了十分钟，在笔记本上写下三行字："一，她借笔，是为了换分。笔是锚。二，不借她我的笔。三，天台那一推，这次让她推个空——还要录下来。"合上本子，她检查笔袋：自己的、备用的，还有一支周雨上周落在她桌上的笔。她把周雨那支，放进了笔袋最外层。

【S05 重生·考场】
最后一科开考前，沈曼果然凑过来："知夏，笔借我用一下，我那支断墨了。"林知夏笑了笑，从最外层抽出那支笔："给。"沈曼接过去，画了两下。林知夏注意到她握笔的姿势很怪，像在感受什么。还笔时，指尖在笔杆上停了半秒——和前世一模一样。林知夏接回来，随手放进笔袋，再没碰过那支笔。

【S06 重演·出分】
查分夜。林知夏输入准考证号，屏幕跳出：669——和她估的分一模一样。她愣了几秒，刷新一遍，还是669。手机响了，周雨的声音发颤："知夏，我查到487。可我估分只有330左右啊！"她握着手机，慢慢笑了，笑到一半又停住。与此同时，班级群炸了：沈曼发了条动态，配图是查分截图——"330。怎么会这样？"评论区沉默了很久，有人小心翼翼地问："曼姐，是不是查错了？"沈曼没有再回。

【S07 周雨的分数】
第二天，周雨冲到林知夏家，眼睛通红："我申请复核了，说我的487分无误。可我明明只估了三百多！"林知夏还没开口，沈曼就推门闯了进来。她抢过周雨的手机，看见487后脱口而出："你明明只有330！"周雨反问："成绩刚出，你怎么知道我原来的分数？"沈曼的脸色一变，摔下手机转身就走。周雨看向林知夏："知夏，你是不是知道什么？"沉默了很久，林知夏开口："那支笔。她借的那支笔，是你的。"周雨愣住："什么笔？""高考那天她借笔。我递给她的，是你的笔。"周雨的脸色慢慢变了："你是故意的？"林知夏没有否认。"所以你保护了自己，把我推出去了？"林知夏："我没想到会这样，我以为她换的是——""你以为？那是我的高考，你以为是什么？"门关上了。林知夏坐在黑暗里，很久没有开灯。

【S08 天台·收网】
当晚，沈曼的电话打进来，声音发抖："是你。你换了我的笔。"林知夏："我没换。我只是没借你我的。""你知不知道我准备了多久？！""周雨准备了更久。"电话那头沉默三秒："你找死。"次日晚八点，天台。林知夏去了：书包带上别着运动相机，袖口藏着录音笔，周雨守在楼梯间——不是为了帮她，是为了亲耳听沈曼承认换分，拿回自己的分数。沈曼上来时，手里举着一支笔："你知道这是什么吗？只要我写下你的名字——""你写。"林知夏打断她，"你换了周雨一次，再换我一次，日志上就是两条记录。服务器删得掉，删不掉离线备份。你只剩一个选择：承认换分，把周雨的分数还回去。"沈曼扑上来。林知夏侧身让开，沈曼收势不住撞上护栏。楼梯间脚步声轰然而至——警察、周雨，还有老宋，被沈家开除的技术员，手里拿着系统日志的打印件。

【S09 日志】
三天前，林知夏就敲开了老宋的门——前世出分那几天，贴吧有个自称统分系统内部人的帖子被秒删，ID她记下来了，这辈子顺藤摸瓜。老宋把打印件摊在桌上：一个无签名的异常模块，代号"易分"，只有两行字段——"借笔人：沈曼。被借笔人：周雨。交换结果：成功。"三天后，多了一条状态："目标错误，建议回退。"林知夏："系统自己知道换错了。"老宋："系统不傻。它只是不关心。"周雨站在门口，声音很轻："所以我的分数，是被它判了'建议忽略'？"老宋没说话，把U盘推了过来。

【S10 庭审】
警方立案。沈曼的律师主张"所谓系统根本不存在"。检方提交了三样东西：老宋的离线日志、天台录音，以及沈曼查分当晚发的那条动态截图。庭审上，法官问沈曼："你查到487分，第一反应为什么是给林知夏打电话，而不是申请复核？"沈曼张了张嘴，什么也没说出来。林知夏坐在旁听席上，没有看她。

【S11 清算】
官方通报：沈曼利用来源不明的"易分系统"非法对调统分数据，涉嫌破坏计算机信息系统、故意杀人未遂，被批准逮捕；其父因三百万买通复核环节同步被查，行贿链条曝光，沈氏集团股价连跌七日、银行抽贷、宣告破产。"易分系统"来源不明，已移交专门机构调查。周雨的分数按卷面恢复。通知书寄到那天，她给林知夏发来一条消息："分数回来了。但我们回不去了。"林知夏盯着屏幕看了很久，回了一个字："嗯。"

【S12 尾声】
林知夏的录取通知寄到那天，母亲把信封擦了三遍才敢拆。手机亮了一下，周雨的消息："我报了师范。以后当老师，不让任何人换学生的分。"林知夏笑了笑，打字回去："我报法学。""为什么？""因为系统还在。总得有人盯着它。"她把那支笔——周雨的那支——放在书桌玻璃板下，旁边压着一张便签："这一次，每一分都是我自己挣的。下一次，谁也别想换走。"但她没有扔掉那支笔。系统没有消失，它只是换了一种方式，在等下一个借笔的人。


## StoryBridge 最终英语稿（原样）

### S01 Past Life · The Borrowed Pen

Before the final certification exam. Outside the testing hall. Lin Zhixia murmurs essay points under her breath. Shen Man walks up: "Zhixia, lend me a pen. Mine just died." Lin Zhixia pulls one from her pencil case and hands it over. Shen Man takes it, scribbles twice on scratch paper, gives it back: "Thanks. This one writes nice." Lin Zhixia doesn't think twice. She doesn't see it — when Shen Man returns the pen, her fingertip rests half a second on the biometric strip along the barrel.

### S02 Past Life · Score Night

Late night. The National Education Data Alliance API portal refreshes. A number pops up: 487. Lin Zhixia refreshes a dozen more times. Her hands shake harder each time. Her mother leans in, looks once, and drops into a chair. Her homeroom teacher, Old Chen, is stunned on the phone: "Impossible. Twenty years teaching — your coursework and project record can't produce an index like that. I'll file a data audit request with the Alliance right now." Three days later, the audit is denied. The reply is four words: Data verified accurate. Meanwhile, Shen Man's Academic Proficiency Index of 669 tops the school. In an interview she says, "I just performed above my usual" — then her eyes go blank for a second. She glances down at the hand that took the pen: a faint blue mark on her palm, fading. The comments all praise her: "Rich and she still works this hard."

### S03 Past Life · The Rooftop

The day college match results lock. An anonymous text lures her to the rooftop. Shen Man drops all pretense: "You think I got here on my dad? His three million bought someone inside the Alliance to plant bad data. But the tampering — the API edit — I did that myself." Lin Zhixia: "You?" Shen Man holds out her hand. The blue mark on her palm is almost gone: "That pen of yours. It writes nice. Guess why I had to borrow yours?" Lin Zhixia turns to leave — to report it, to call the police. Footsteps close behind. Two hands slam into her back. Through the rushing wind, Shen Man's voice drifts down from the rooftop edge: "Next life, don't lend your pen to just anyone." What the world called a suicide was a murder from start to finish.

### S04 Rebirth · The Switch

She opens her eyes. It's the first morning of the APC certification exam. The admission ticket on the desk, her mother's porridge at the door, the date on her phone — exactly as she remembers. Lin Zhixia pinches the back of her hand. It hurts. She sits for ten minutes, then writes three lines in her notebook: "One. She borrows the pen to alter data. The pen is the anchor. Two. Don't lend her mine. Three. That push on the rooftop — this time she hits air. And I record it." She closes the notebook and checks her pencil case: her own pen, a backup, and one Zhou Yu left on her desk last week. She slides Zhou Yu's pen into the outermost pocket.

### S05 Rebirth · The Testing Hall

Before the final certification exam. Shen Man walks up, just as expected: "Zhixia, lend me a pen. Mine's out of ink." Lin Zhixia smiles and pulls the pen from the outer pocket: "Here." Shen Man takes it, scribbles twice on scratch paper. Lin Zhixia notices her grip is strange — like she's feeling for something. The biometric reader on the barrel presses against Shen Man's palm. Her thumb rubs the inside of the clip, over and over, as if confirming a contact point. When she returns it, her fingertip rests half a second on the barrel — identical to last time. Lin Zhixia takes it back, drops it in her case, and never touches it again.

### S06 Replay · Score Release

API release night. Lin Zhixia logs into the National Education Data Alliance certification portal, enters her student ID. The screen reads: 669 — exactly her own estimate. She stares for a few seconds, refreshes. Still 669. Her phone rings. Zhou Yu's voice shakes: "Zhixia, I got 487. But my estimate was around 330!" Lin Zhixia grips the phone, smiles slowly — then stops mid-smile. Meanwhile, the class group explodes: Shen Man posts a screenshot of her API result — "330. How is this possible?" The comments go quiet for a long time. Someone finally asks, carefully: "Man, is the system wrong?" Shen Man doesn't reply.

### S07 Zhou Yu's Score

The next day, Zhou Yu rushes into Lin Zhixia's home, eyes red: "I filed a data audit with the Alliance. They said my 487 is accurate. But I estimated barely over three hundred!" Before Lin Zhixia can speak, Shen Man pushes through the door. She snatches Zhou Yu's phone, sees 487, and blurts out: "You only had 330!" Zhou Yu fires back: "The API just dropped. How do you know my original index?" Shen Man's face changes. She slams the phone down and walks out. Zhou Yu turns to Lin Zhixia: "Zhixia. You know something." Long silence. Then Lin Zhixia speaks: "That pen. The one she borrowed. It was yours." Zhou Yu freezes: "What pen?" "Exam day. She borrowed a pen. I handed her yours." Zhou Yu's face shifts slowly: "You did it on purpose?" Lin Zhixia doesn't deny it. "So you protected yourself and pushed me out?" Lin Zhixia: "I didn't think it would go this way. I thought she was swapping—" "You thought? That's my Academic Proficiency Index. What do you mean, you thought?" The door slams. Lin Zhixia sits in the dark. She doesn't turn on the light for a long time.

### S08 Rooftop · The Trap

That night, Shen Man calls. Her voice trembles: "It was you. You switched my pen." Lin Zhixia: "I didn't switch anything. I just didn't lend you mine." "Do you know how long I prepared?!" "Zhou Yu prepared longer." Three seconds of silence. Then: "You're dead." Next night, eight p.m. The rooftop. Lin Zhixia comes prepared: an action camera clipped to her backpack strap, a voice recorder in her sleeve. Zhou Yu waits in the stairwell — not to help, but to hear Shen Man admit the tampering herself and get her admission index back. Shen Man steps out holding a pen: "You know what this is? The moment I write your name—" "Write it." Lin Zhixia cuts her off. "You borrowed Zhou Yu's pen and triggered one EduSwap. Borrow mine and trigger another. That's two tampering records in the offline log. The server can delete them. Old Song's offline backup can't be deleted. You have one choice: admit you used EduSwap to alter the data and give Zhou Yu her score back." Shen Man lunges. Lin Zhixia sidesteps. Shen Man can't stop herself and slams into the railing. Footsteps thunder up the stairwell — police, Zhou Yu, and Old Song, the EduSwap module technician the Shen family fired, holding printed offline logs.

### S09 The Log

Three days earlier, Lin Zhixia knocked on Old Song's door. In her past life, during score week, a forum post from someone claiming to be inside the college admissions data system was deleted within seconds. She memorized the user ID. This time, she followed the trail. Old Song spreads printouts on the table: an unsigned anomaly module, codename "EduSwap." Two fields only — "Borrower: Shen Man. Lent-from: Zhou Yu. Swap result: Success." Three days later, a new line appears: "Wrong target. Rollback recommended." Lin Zhixia: "The system knows it swapped the wrong person." Old Song: "The system isn't stupid. It just doesn't care." Zhou Yu stands in the doorway, voice barely audible: "So my score was flagged 'recommended ignore'?" Old Song says nothing. He slides the USB drive across the table.

### S10 The Trial

Police open a case. Shen Man's lawyer argues: "The so-called EduSwap module doesn't exist." The prosecution submits three items: Old Song's offline logs, the rooftop recording, and a screenshot of Shen Man's post from the night she saw her API score. In court, the judge asks Shen Man: "You saw 330. Your first reaction was to call Lin Zhixia — not to file a data audit with the Alliance. Why?" Shen Man opens her mouth. Nothing comes out. Lin Zhixia sits in the gallery. She doesn't look at her.

### S11 The Reckoning

Official statement: Shen Man used the illegally implanted "EduSwap" module to tamper with college admissions data. Charges include unauthorized modification of a computer information system and attempted murder. She is arrested. Her father is investigated for bribing insiders at the National Education Data Alliance to plant false data in the Academic Proficiency Certification system. The bribery chain is exposed. The Shen Group's stock falls for seven straight days, banks call in their loans, and the company declares bankruptcy. The EduSwap module's origin is unknown. It has been handed to a specialized agency for investigation. Zhou Yu's score is restored based on her exam paper. The day her admission letter arrives, she sends Lin Zhixia a message: "My score is back. But we can't go back." Lin Zhixia stares at the screen for a long time. She replies with one word: "Yeah."

### S12 Epilogue

The day Lin Zhixia's admission letter arrives, her mother wipes the envelope three times before opening it. Her phone lights up. A message from Zhou Yu: "I applied to teacher's college. When I'm a teacher, no one swaps a student's score." Lin Zhixia smiles and types back: "I applied to law school." "Why?" "Because the system is still out there. Someone has to watch it." She places the pen — Zhou Yu's pen — under the glass on her desk. Beside it, a sticky note: "This time, every point is mine. Next time, no one takes them." But she doesn't throw the pen away. The system hasn't disappeared. It's just changed its shape, waiting for the next person who lends a pen.

**S08 — THE ROOF, PART TWO**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LYDIA'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**

<center>MADELINE (V.O.)</center>

<center>LYDIA</center>

Lydia sidesteps. Madeline stumbles. Hits the railing.

Footsteps thunder up the stairs. Police. Zoe. And ARTHUR SLOAN, 60s, former data engineer for the National Education Data Alliance — holding a stack of printed logs.

**S09 — THE LOG**

**INT. ARTHUR SLOAN'S APARTMENT — THREE DAYS EARLIER**

<center>LYDIA</center>

<center>LYDIA</center>

<center>ARTHUR</center>

Zoe stands in the doorway. Voice barely audible.

<center>ZOE</center>

**S12 — CODA**

**INT. CHEN HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Lydia's college acceptance packet arrives. Diane wipes the envelope three times before opening it.

*"Why?"*

*"Because the system's still out there. Someone has to watch it."*

She takes the pen — Zoe's pen — and places it under the glass on her desk. Beside it, a sticky note:

*"This time, every point was mine. Next time, no one takes them."*

But she doesn't throw the pen away.

The system didn't disappear. It just changed shape. And it's waiting for the next person who lends a pen.
