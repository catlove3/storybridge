# NOT MY RECORD

## A Short Drama in Seven Scenes

### Adapted for a U.S. Setting

---

**ADAPTATION NOTES (for production):**

- **Setting:** A mid-sized American city, present day. All universities, clubs, and regattas are fictional.
- **Xiaoning → NINA ZHAO**, 17, a high school senior who loves to draw. She has never rowed.
- **Father → DAVID ZHAO**, late 40s, first-generation immigrant, works long hours, believes an Ivy League acceptance is the only proof his family has made it.
- **Xiaolan → JULES PARK**, Nina's classmate and friend, a student aide in the athletics office.
- **The Broker → RICKY SALAZAR**, a "college admissions consultant" who promises athletic recruitment slots.
- **The scam:** Ricky fabricates a rowing résumé for Nina — a fake club registration, a Photoshopped crew-team photo with Nina's face pasted onto another rower's body, and a forged "Regional Regatta Entry Confirmation" bearing her name. The promise: "You don't need to show up. We arrange the recruit slot."
- **What Nina misses:** A rowing recruit showcase (not an exam) — she's hospitalized after a fall.
- **Key evidence chain:** Jules brings the real club roster and seat assignments; cross-referenced with Ricky's photo and Nina's hospital records, it proves Nina was never in the boat.
- **No magic database, no certificate that guarantees admission.** The fraud is exposed through documents, photos, and a hospital log — all filmable.

## S01 — THE SHOWCASE SHE MISSED

**INT. HOSPITAL ROOM — DAY**

NINA ZHAO, 17, lies in a hospital bed, right leg in a cast, sketchbook open on her lap. A half-finished drawing of a rowing shell on the water. She's not happy with it.

Her father, DAVID ZHAO, stands by the window, phone pressed to his ear. He's been on hold for a while.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (into phone)
> Yes, I understand the showcase was yesterday. She was in a hospital bed. There has to be —

He listens. His jaw tightens.

<center>NINA</center>
> Dad. It's fine.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (covering the phone)
> It's not fine. This was the one day the coaches were going to be there. The one day.

He hangs up. Stares out the window.

<center>NINA</center>
> I never rowed anyway. I draw.

<center>DAVID</center>
> You draw. And you'll draw at a good school. But you get into a good school through a door. This was a door.

The door opens. RICKY SALAZAR, 40s, polo shirt, tablet under his arm, steps in like he owns the hallway.

<center>RICKY</center>
> Mr. Zhao? Ricky Salazar. We spoke on the phone.

David shakes his hand too eagerly.

<center>RICKY</center>
> (glancing at Nina)
> This is the athlete?

<center>NINA</center>
> I'm not an athlete.

<center>RICKY</center>
> (smiling)
> That's what the file says. And the file is what schools read.

He turns to David, lowers his voice — but not enough.

<center>RICKY</center>
> Here's the situation. The showcase is done. But recruit slots? Those are arranged. You don't need to be in the boat. You need to be in the paperwork.

<center>DAVID</center>
> What does that mean?

<center>RICKY</center>
> It means the door is still open. But it closes fast.

Nina watches them. Her pencil stops moving.

<center>NINA</center>
> How do I try out?

Ricky doesn't answer her. He's already scrolling his tablet, talking to David.

<center>RICKY</center>
> I'll need a retainer to start the file. We move quickly.

<center>NINA</center>
> (louder)
> How do I try out?

Ricky looks at her. A beat. Then back to David.

<center>RICKY</center>
> She's got spirit. Coaches love spirit.

He doesn't answer the question.

## S02 — A RECORD THAT ISN'T HERS

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — NIGHT**

A small kitchen. Dishes drying. David sits at the table, laptop open. Nina limps in on crutches.

David's phone buzzes. He opens an email. His face changes.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Nina. Look at this.

He turns the laptop toward her. On screen:

- A **"Regional Regatta Entry Confirmation"** — Nina Zhao's name, a date, a boat number.
- A **photo**: a crew team on the water, eight rowers, one face clearly Nina's — but the body is wrong, the posture is wrong, the lighting doesn't match.
- A **club registration record** from "Cedar Ridge Rowing Club."

<center>NINA</center>
> That's not me.

<center>DAVID</center>
> It has your name.

<center>NINA</center>
> Dad. Those two days? I was in the hospital. You were there. You slept in that chair.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (quiet)
> I know.

He keeps looking at the confirmation. The date. The boat number.

<center>DAVID</center>
> But look at this. A regional regatta. That's a real event. If a coach sees this —

<center>NINA</center>
> Sees what? A photo of someone else's body with my face on it?

<center>DAVID</center>
> Don't say that.

<center>NINA</center>
> I'm saying it because it's true.

David closes the laptop. Opens it again. He can't stop looking.

<center>DAVID</center>
> We can't ask questions yet. If we ask questions, they pull the file. And then it's gone. You understand? Gone.

Nina stares at him. Then she takes out her phone. Photographs the screen. The confirmation. The photo. The registration.

<center>DAVID</center>
> What are you doing?

<center>NINA</center>
> Keeping a copy.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Nina —

<center>NINA</center>
> You told me to look. I'm looking.

She saves everything. Her thumb hovers over the photo — the fake crew team. She zooms in on her own pasted face.

<center>NINA</center>
> That's not even my jawline.

## S03 — WHAT JULES REMEMBERED

**INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY — DAY**

Nina at her locker. JULES PARK, 17, walks up, holding a yearbook.

<center>JULES</center>
> You left this in the art room. Again.

<center>NINA</center>
> Thanks.

Jules doesn't leave. She's looking at Nina strangely.

<center>JULES</center>
> Hey. Weird question. Did you ever row? Like, ever?

<center>NINA</center>
> I can barely walk right now. What do you think?

<center>JULES</center>
> That's what I thought. Because I work in the athletics office, right? Filing. And I was organizing the crew team's paperwork from last season.

She opens the yearbook. Inside, folded between pages, is a **crew team roster and seat assignment sheet** — a photocopy.

<center>JULES</center>
> This is the real roster. From the regatta your name is on.

She points to a seat number.

<center>JULES</center>
> Seat four. That's the seat in the photo with your face. But look who's listed.

The name on the roster: **MEGAN LIU**.

<center>NINA</center>
> I don't know her.

<center>JULES</center>
> She graduated. But I remember her. She was in my bio class. And I remember thinking — that seat photo? The girl in it? That's Megan. Not you.

Nina takes the roster. Studies it.

<center>NINA</center>
> Do you still have the original? The full packet?

<center>JULES</center>
> I can get it. Why? What's going on?

<center>NINA</center>
> I need to see everything. Before I say anything.

Jules nods. Nina opens the yearbook — and there, on the inside back cover, is a **self-portrait Nina drew months ago**. She traces it with her finger.

<center>NINA</center>
> This is the only thing in here that's actually mine.

## S04 — ANOTHER PAYMENT

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — NIGHT**

David on the phone. Ricky's voice on speaker.

<center>RICKY (V.O.)</center>
> The file's flagged for verification. It happens. We need a coordination fee to push it through. Five thousand.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Five thousand? I already paid —

<center>RICKY (V.O.)</center>
> And you want the slot or not? Because if someone starts asking questions — if the school starts pulling records — the kid's future is done. You understand me? Done.

David's hand shakes. He opens a loan app on his phone. Starts typing.

Nina limps in. Sees the screen.

<center>NINA</center>
> Dad. Stop.

<center>DAVID</center>
> It's the last payment.

<center>NINA</center>
> There's no such thing as the last payment. Not with him.

She puts her hand over his phone. He looks at her.

<center>NINA</center>
> Before you borrow one more dollar — find out whose record this actually is.

A long beat. David looks at the loan app. At his daughter.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (quiet)
> I don't know how to do this.

<center>NINA</center>
> Then we figure it out together.

## S05 — SHE DECIDES TO EXPOSE IT

**INT. NINA'S BEDROOM — DAY**

Nina's bed. On it: the **original crew packet** from Jules — roster, seat assignments, race schedule. Next to it: Nina's **hospital discharge summary**, dated the same weekend as the regatta. Next to that: Ricky's **Photoshopped photo** and the **forged entry confirmation**.

Jules sits on the floor. David stands in the doorway.

<center>JULES</center>
> Okay. Seat four. Regatta date. Megan Liu.

She puts the real roster next to Ricky's photo.

<center>JULES</center>
> Same boat. Same seat. Same race. But the face is different. He pasted Nina's face onto Megan's body.

<center>DAVID</center>
> How can you be sure?

<center>NINA</center>
> Because of this.

She holds up the hospital discharge summary.

<center>NINA</center>
> I was admitted Friday at 4 p.m. I was discharged Sunday at 11 a.m. The regatta was Saturday. I was in a bed with a broken leg. Not in a boat.

She lays the discharge summary next to the photo.

<center>NINA</center>
> The girl in this photo is sitting in a boat. I was sitting in a hospital bed. That's not a mistake. That's a substitution.

David picks up the photo. Looks at it for a long time.

<center>DAVID</center>
> He was going to send someone else in your place.

<center>NINA</center>
> He already did. He just put my name on it.

She takes the photo from her father. Looks at her own pasted face.

<center>NINA</center>
> You bought me a future. The person inside it isn't even me.

David sits down. He looks old.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I was scared. You missed the showcase. I thought — if I don't fix this, I failed you.

<center>NINA</center>
> You didn't fail me. You just believed him.

<center>DAVID</center>
> What do we do?

<center>NINA</center>
> We stop using it. We tell the truth. And we keep everything.

She starts photographing each document. Jules helps.

<center>NINA</center>
> Every promise he made. Every payment. Every fake page. We save it all.

## S06 — WALKING AWAY FROM THE SHORTCUT

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — NIGHT**

David at the kitchen table. Nina across from him. On the table: printed emails, payment receipts, the forged documents, the real roster.

David's phone shows the loan app — **"Confirm Transfer"** button glowing.

He looks at it. Then at Nina.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I was going to borrow against the car.

<center>NINA</center>
> I know.

<center>DAVID</center>
> For a photo of someone else's body.

<center>NINA</center>
> Yeah.

He closes the app. Sets the phone face down.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I'll call the schools tomorrow. Tell them everything.

<center>NINA</center>
> I'll be there.

<center>DAVID</center>
> They might —

<center>NINA</center>
> I know what they might do. But I'd rather they know my real name than a fake one.

David nods. Then he reaches behind him and picks up something from the counter: **Nina's sketchbook**.

He slides it across the table to her.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Then we start with what you can actually do.

Nina opens the sketchbook. The half-finished rowing shell from the hospital. She turns the page. A portrait of her father. Then another. Then the self-portrait from the yearbook.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I never really looked at these.

<center>NINA</center>
> You were busy looking at doors.

<center>DAVID</center>
> (quiet)
> Show me.

## S07 — HER OWN NAME

**INT. ZHAO APARTMENT — DAY — WEEKS LATER**

Sunlight. Nina at the table, sketchbook open, working. Her cast is off. She moves easily now.

David comes in, holding the mail. He stops.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Nina.

She looks up. He holds out an envelope.

She takes it. Opens it. Reads.

Her face changes — not shock. Something quieter.

<center>NINA</center>
> It's an interview. For the art program. They want to see my portfolio.

<center>DAVID</center>
> Just an interview?

<center>NINA</center>
> Just an interview. No slot. No file. No one else's body.

She sets the letter on the table. Next to it, she places the **self-portrait** from the yearbook.

<center>NINA</center>
> This time, the name on it and the work inside — they're both mine.

David sits down. Pulls the yearbook toward him. Opens it. For the first time, he turns the pages slowly. The drawings. The self-portraits. The half-finished rowing shell. The portrait of him.

He stops on the self-portrait. Looks at it. Then at his daughter.

<center>DAVID</center>
> This is good.

<center>NINA</center>
> I know.

<center>DAVID</center>
> I mean it.

<center>NINA</center>
> I know you do.

She goes back to her sketchbook. He keeps turning pages.

**FADE OUT.**
