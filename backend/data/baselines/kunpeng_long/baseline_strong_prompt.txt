**SCRIPT: "THE REBIRTH OF SHADOW"**

---

**[SCENE 01]**

*FADE IN:*

*INT. LUXURY BEDROOM - NIGHT*

A figure snaps awake. Before his eyes even open, his hand slams down instinctively, propelling his body upward—a combat reflex honed by years of lethal precision.

*THUD.*

He crashes back down onto something impossibly soft.

His eyes fly open. Panic. Confusion.

He's lying on a massive bed—ridiculously massive. A California King on steroids. It could comfortably sleep eight people.

The room is lavishly decorated. Antique furniture. Rich fabrics. But empty. Just a table, the bed, and him.

*What the hell?*

He was in the middle of a firefight. Bullets. Explosions. His body failing at the worst possible moment.

Now he's here. In silk sheets.

His mind clings to the last shred of memory from his previous life...

---

**[SCENE 02]**

His name was Kai. A ghost. The most lethal assassin in the world.

Five years. Zero failures. A perfect record that had never been achieved before—and never would be again.

The underworld knew him only as "The Shadow." A name whispered in fear across every continent. The top spot on the global assassination ranking. The top spot on the global bounty list—a $100 million price tag on his head, untouched for three years running.

Many had tried to claim it. All of them were dead.

Two legendary hitmen—rivals who had once matched his reputation—accepted the contract on the same day. Forty-eight hours later, they were found in separate countries, in separate ways, equally dead.

After that, no one dared. The bounty kept climbing, but the message was clear: The Shadow was untouchable.

No one knew his face. No one knew his name. He worked alone. Always. He had no friends. No partners. No attachments.

And he was... particular.

He chose his clients as carefully as his targets. If he didn't like you, no amount of money could buy his services—even for the easiest job imaginable.

---

**[SCENE 03]**

But if he decided someone deserved to die? He'd handle it himself. No contract. No payment. Then he'd show up at the victim's enemy's doorstep and demand compensation.

One time, he eliminated a notorious human trafficker—a monster who had destroyed dozens of lives. But there was no one to pay him. So he found one of the rescued victims, a young girl, and collected a single dollar from her.

"I never work for free," he said, dead serious. "Not even once."

His mentor and fellow operatives had long since given up trying to understand him.

There was the infamous bathroom incident. He'd removed all the toilet paper from his mentor's private restroom, then charged him $50,000 to deliver a single roll—while broadcasting the whole humiliating exchange to every member of their organization, plus a few hired models for maximum embarrassment.

But his greatest flaw, by his own admission? He had too much heart.

As an assassin. A killer. A man with blood on his hands.

People laughed when he said it. But he meant it.

He couldn't stand bullies. Couldn't stand the powerful crushing the weak. And above all, he couldn't stand anyone—anyone—preying on his countrymen.

That "patriotic" streak had gotten him into more trouble than any contract ever had.

Yet clients still lined up. Why?

---

**[SCENE 04]**

Because he was perfect.

Deadly accurate with any firearm. Lethal in hand-to-hand combat. Master of every weapon known to man.

But the real reason? One hundred percent completion rate. Unprecedented. Untouchable. The ultimate professional.

He was the apex predator of the assassin world. The only operative in history to never—not once—fail a mission.

But underneath all that ice-cold professionalism? He was a patriot. A true believer.

And that's what got him killed.

Word reached him: a foreign intelligence agency had stolen a priceless ancient artifact from an archaeological dig in his homeland. A sacred relic. A national treasure. And they'd smuggled it out before his country's security forces even knew it was missing.

He was furious.

He went in alone. Against nearly a hundred armed operatives. He killed seventy-three of them—through ambushes, traps, and finally, open combat. The survivors were so terrified they'd lost all will to fight. He could have walked away. Easily.

But when his hand touched the artifact—a small, exquisitely carved jade pagoda, no bigger than his palm—something went wrong.

His wounded hand touched the ancient stone, and suddenly his entire body seized. Paralysis. Complete. Total. He couldn't even blink.

Blood from his wounds flowed into the pagoda, soaking into the jade as if it were drinking.

His last sight: fifty micro-grenades arcing toward him. Twenty automatic weapons opening fire.

And he couldn't move a single muscle.

---

**[SCENE 05]**

*He was going to die here.*

*But you know what? He didn't regret a thing.*

*In his life, he'd killed corrupt officials, brutal gangsters, foreign agents who deserved what they got. Over a thousand in total. Every single one had earned their death.*

*He'd lived hard. Lived free. Lived on his own terms.*

*"If this is hell," he thought, "then I'll walk in with my head high."*

*"I killed the guilty. I cleansed the filth. I did what needed to be done."*

*"And I'd do it all again."*

He laughed out loud—a defiant, triumphant sound in the face of death.

"Sir? S-Sir? Are you... are you alright?"

A small, trembling voice. Female. Young.

A cool hand pressed against his forehead.

*Sir?*

---

**[SCENE 06]**

His eyes snapped open.

*Not dead. Not in hell. Not dreaming.*

A flood of foreign memories crashed into his mind—a lifetime of images, sounds, emotions that weren't his. A different name. A different face. A different world.

He was in someone else's body.

Reborn. Reincarnated. Whatever you wanted to call it.

But his own memories—the assassin, the kills, the life—were still there. Vivid. Complete.

*How is this possible?*

He stared at the ceiling, processing. A hand waved nervously in front of his face.

"Sir? Please... you're scaring me..."

He burst into laughter. Raw. Joyful. Unhinged.

"Holy shit! I'm alive! I'm actually alive! All those good deeds finally paid off! Karma's real, baby!"

The girl—a young maid, maybe twelve years old—shrieked and scrambled backward, pressing herself against the wall like a terrified bird.

Then he shrieked too.

His voice. High. Reedy. Almost feminine.

*Oh no. Oh no no no no.*

His hand flew to his crotch.

*...Still there. Thank God.*

He exhaled in relief. The maid stared at him like he'd lost his mind.

He looked at his new body. Weak. Soft. Muscles atrophied. Joints stiff. This guy was in terrible shape.

---

**[SCENE 07]**

*But that's fine. Give me a few years. I'll be back on top.*

He pushed the thought aside and took stock of his situation.

This wasn't his world. The furniture, the clothes, the customs—everything was different. He was a stranger here. Alone. No allies. No resources. No idea how anything worked.

Even for a man with his mental fortitude, the weight of it pressed down on him.

He'd been given a second chance. A miracle. But at what cost?

He'd lost everything. His homeland. His purpose. His identity.

For the first time in his life, he felt like a ghost in a world that wasn't his.

His eyes burned. His throat tightened.

*I thought I could let go. I thought I had nothing left to lose.*

*But I was wrong.*

*I had so much. And now it's all gone.*

---

**[SCENE 08]**

He closed his eyes. A single tear slid down his cheek.

*This is the first time I've cried in two lifetimes.*

He looked at his reflection in a bronze mirror. Young face. Sharp features. Thin lips. Long eyebrows slanting upward. Eyes that were narrow, intense, calculating.

"Not bad," he muttered. "But a little too pretty. Too soft."

He thought of his old face. Unremarkable. Forgettable. The face of a man who could disappear into any crowd.

*And now I'm stuck in this pretty boy's body.*

He noticed a small mark on his wrist—a tattoo-like pattern. A tiny pagoda.

The artifact. It had come with him.

He touched it gently, and something stirred in his chest. Pride. Defiance.

*At least I kept it out of their hands.*

*It's still in the hands of my people.*

The mark began to glow—a soft, golden light. Then warmth flooded through him. His head swam. And suddenly, something appeared in his mind. A presence. A connection.

The mark on his wrist vanished.

"What the hell?" He blinked, shaking his head. "First it's a statue, then a tattoo, now it's... in my head?"

*Is this thing some kind of ancient relic?*

"Sir? The Master requests your presence."

He looked up. The maid stood in the doorway, trembling.

"Requests?" He raised an eyebrow. "Why should I go just because he asks?"

*Who does he think he is? My grandfather?*

...Wait. He was. Technically.

"Um... I don't know, sir. He just said to bring you."

The girl looked ready to bolt at any moment. Her eyes were wide with fear.

*What did this body's original owner do to her?*

---

**[SCENE 09]**

He sighed and swung his legs off the bed.

"Fine. Lead the way."

*Let's see what this world has in store for me.*

*I'm starting from zero. New body. New world. New rules.*

*But I'm still me.*

*And I've never failed at anything in my life.*

*This won't be any different.*

He followed the maid out of the room, his new body moving awkwardly, his mind already planning.

*First step: figure out where I am.*

*Second step: get this body into shape.*

*Third step: find out who needs killing.*

He smiled—a cold, dangerous smile that didn't reach his eyes.

*Some things never change.*

*And some things are about to.*

---

**[END OF EPISODE 1]**
