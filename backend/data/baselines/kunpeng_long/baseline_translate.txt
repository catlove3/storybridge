**[SCENE 01]**
**Chapter 1**

Jun Xie suddenly woke up.
Without even opening his eyes, his right hand instinctively slammed against the ground, propelling himself upward.
This was a dangerous place; life hung by a thread. He couldn't stay!
That was his first thought upon waking—the instinctive reflex of an elite assassin honed to perfection!

As his body leaped into the air, his arm suddenly gave way. It couldn't support his own weight at all. With a heavy *thud*, he crashed back down!

For a moment, Jun Xie was utterly horrified. What was going on?!
Then he quickly realized that beneath him was a soft bed!

Looking around, he found himself in a lavishly decorated room.
It was, however, strangely empty. Apart from a square table, the only other thing was himself lying on a "giant bed." And it truly was a *giant* bed.
It was enormous, big enough for seven or eight people to sleep on without feeling crowded!

What is this?
Wasn't I in the middle of a fight?
How did I end up in bed??
Jun Xie's thoughts were still stuck in the moment before he lost consciousness, or rather... temporarily lingering on the last thoughts of his previous life!

---

**[SCENE 02]**

Jun Xie was an assassin, and an exceptionally brilliant one at that—a gold-medal assassin. Since his debut, he had been undefeated for five years, with a success rate of a perfect one hundred percent, unprecedented in history!

This made him the number one assassin on the killer charts, and the name "Evil Lord" consequently topped the world's underworld rankings!

He held another number one distinction as well: the bounty on his head had firmly occupied the top spot on the world's most-wanted list for three years straight!!

Many first-rate assassins had accepted this contract over the years, but they all ended up dead, while "Evil Lord" continued to live!

A wealthy man from Country Y offered a hundred million dollars for "Evil Lord's" head. Two assassins, both world-class top-tier killers who were once considered his equals, accepted the job. Within three days, they both met untimely deaths.

After that, no one was willing to take on this death sentence. Everyone gave him a wide berth. Even though the bounty was repeatedly increased, no one ever dared to claim it.

What good is money, no matter how wonderful, if you don't have your life to enjoy it!

The name "Evil Lord" became a taboo on the underworld bounty list!
And the name "Evil Lord" struck fear into the hearts of underworld figures across the globe!

Many knew of the man "Evil Lord," but not a single person knew what this "Evil Lord," this King of Assassins, actually looked like, or what kind of person he truly was!

Jun Xie's personality was exactly as his name suggested: one word—eccentric! Two words—very eccentric! Three words—extremely eccentric!

He always acted alone, never partnering with anyone.
He didn't have a single friend!

Moreover, when taking jobs, he not only considered the client but also scrutinized the target!
If he didn't like the client's look, even if they offered a fortune to kill a defenseless beggar, he would refuse without hesitation.

---

**[SCENE 03]**

But if he saw someone who deserved to die, he would take action automatically.
Then he would go to that person's enemy's doorstep to demand payment, and refusing to pay was not an option.
And often, this family had never even hired him, nor had they ever even heard of him...

Rumor had it... once, he killed a despicable human trafficker but couldn't find the victim. With no other choice, he asked a little girl who had been trafficked for a one-cent coin, and even said with conviction: "I've never done a job without getting paid, and I'm not about to start now..."

This personality of his left his master and fellow disciples, who knew him well, utterly speechless...

Legend had it... once, he removed all the toilet paper from the bathroom beforehand. When his master went to use the squat toilet and found no paper, he asked Jun Xie to bring him some. Jun Xie seized the opportunity to demand a fifty-thousand-dollar service fee. His master had no choice but to comply...

As for the reason...
It was because that day, he had called all his fellow disciples to the bathroom door and even invited a few beautiful women to watch...

But he believed his biggest flaw was that he was simply too compassionate.
As an assassin, and a top-tier one at that, with blood on his hands, this statement had made countless people sneer with disdain!

His claim wasn't entirely baseless.
Back home, he couldn't stand the rich bullying the poor, especially officials oppressing commoners. Abroad, he couldn't stand anyone bullying his fellow countrymen!

Because of this "patriotic" personality of his, he had stirred up no end of massive trouble.

But even so, people wanting to hire him still flocked to him like ducks to water!
Why?

---

**[SCENE 04]**

Not only was his marksmanship superb, never missing his target, but he also possessed martial arts skills that were unfathomable and awe-inspiring!
Whether it was fist, palm, sword, or blade, he had cultivated a remarkable level of skill!

However, the biggest reason remained his one hundred percent mission completion rate!
This achievement was truly unprecedented!

He was the undisputed ultimate assassin in the killer world!
He was also, to this day, the only peak-level powerhouse among assassins worldwide who had never once failed a mission!

But, deep down, this gold-medal assassin was actually a typical hot-headed nationalist!

And this time, he had volunteered again. He heard that spies from Country M had unearthed a priceless secret treasure in the Kunlun Mountains of Country Z and had smuggled it back before National Security even got wind of it. So Jun Xie, this typical hothead, got angry!

How could a treasure of the great land of China, especially in this peaceful era, remain in the hands of those from Country M?!

Jun Xie charged in alone, arrogantly taking on nearly a hundred M-country agents. From stealthy assassinations to a final face-to-face brawl, after killing over seventy people, he finally got his hands on the treasure. By that point, the M-country agents were so terrified they had lost their will to fight. If he had wanted to leave, he could have walked away calmly!

And Jun Xie was absolutely confident in that!

But just as his hand touched the treasure—a delicate pagoda only the size of a palm—an extremely unexpected supernatural event occurred. His injured hand grabbed the small pagoda, and suddenly he felt his entire body go numb. He couldn't move a muscle, couldn't even blink!

He didn't notice that the blood flowing from his wound was continuously seeping into the small pagoda—that exquisite, delicate, and strangely ominous little pagoda...

In his final memory, he saw no fewer than fifty micro-grenades flying towards him, and over twenty various firearms spitting fire at him. Yet, despite all his skills, despite having the power to slaughter them all in one go, he couldn't move a single inch. It was maddening!

---

**[SCENE 05]**

"Never thought I, Jun Xie, who roamed the world without a worthy opponent, would die so unjustly here. But I guess I didn't lose out. In this life, the corrupt officials, tyrants, bullies, and foreign agents I've killed number over a thousand. That's enough. Worth it!"

Others smile in the afterlife; I'll smile as I enter hell!
This life, I lived it with vigor, lived it with panache!
I have a clear conscience!

Sure, I killed a lot of people, but not one of them didn't deserve to die!
If that's the case, then I killed them without regret! Even if I go to hell for it, so what?!

Kill, kill, kill, kill, kill!
Kill all the filth!
Wash away all the evil!

Even if I am a despised assassin, so what?!
In this vast world, who else could live as freely as I did? With such satisfaction?!

"Hahaha..." Thinking this, Jun Xie couldn't help but laugh out loud with pride.

"Young Master... are you... are you alright?" A timid voice sounded from beside him, seemingly frightened by his outburst, on the verge of tears.

Then a small, cold hand touched his forehead.

Young Master?

---

**[SCENE 06]**

Am I not dreaming right now?
Am I not in hell either?!
Jun Xie shuddered and snapped his eyes open.

Suddenly, a wave of unfamiliar memories surged up from the depths of his mind!
Strange memories flooded his consciousness like a tidal wave.

Jun Xie was stunned, as if struck by lightning!

I'm in another body?
Reincarnated again?

But why are my past life's memories still so vivid?
Did I not drink the Soup of Forgetfulness?!
Or is this possession of a corpse?!

Either I've time-traveled,
Or I've been reborn into another's body?!

Jun Xie stared blankly, his eyes wide, unable to comprehend the situation for a long while, not moving a muscle.

As the small hand beside him waved frantically in front of his eyes, Jun Xie suddenly shouted with wild joy: "Damn it!

---

**[SCENE 07]**

Good deeds really do get rewarded!
No matter what happened, the point is I'm not dead! Such a great thing! I must have accumulated a ton of good karma in my past life. Immeasurable merit, I bet!?!
Wahahaha..."

A startled shriek came from beside him. A little girl, about ten years old, trembling, shrank away to the side. Her pretty, big eyes blinked in panic, staring fixedly at the "young master" who seemed like a nightmare before her. Her small frame shook, her face growing paler, like a little quail that had been severely frightened.

Another startled shriek, this one shrill and piercing, came from Jun Xie's own mouth.
Because he suddenly realized his voice was high-pitched and sharp, like a girl's. Could it be that his... you know... was gone? No way!

Jun Xie's first reaction, disregarding all decorum, ignoring the little girl in front of him, was to grab desperately at his crotch.

Finally feeling that familiar lump, Jun Xie let out a long sigh of relief. Heaven hadn't been too cruel to him after all; it was still there.
That scared me half to death! I thought I'd transmigrated into some girl's body...

Jun Xie wiped the cold sweat from his brow.

Steadying himself, Jun Xie began to examine his new body.
Meridians were blocked, muscles were flabby, joints were stiff...

How did this guy manage to live like this?
His body is really weak!
This is just terrible!

---

**[SCENE 08]**

Jun Xie muttered to himself, but it didn't matter. As long as the meridians weren't shattered, give me three to five years, and I'll be standing at the top of the world again!

Having made up his mind, Jun Xie then realized that his current surroundings seemed to be a completely unfamiliar world!
This didn't look anything like the Earth he knew!
Here, he was truly alone, knowing no one, understanding nothing, knowing nothing about anything!

What are the rules of this world?
What does this world have?

After pondering all these questions, even with the mental fortitude of this cold-blooded assassin and Evil Lord, he felt a sense of bewilderment.

Looking at the antique furniture and bed, and the clothes that were completely unlike anything from his own era, the initial joy of discovering he wasn't dead and had time-traveled slowly subsided. In its place rose a wave of utter confusion...

So, it's true... I can live again...

This thought, which should have been exhilarating, was instantly followed by a surge of immense loss and pain from the depths of his heart—a subtle feeling of being a rootless duckweed. It made his nose tingle, his eyes sting, and his chest feel tight. Jun Xie curled his lips in self-mockery. The man who had barely shed a tear in his life nearly wept.

It's hard to part with one's homeland; hard to leave one's native soil!
I thought I could be so carefree, thought I could let go easily. But when it came down to it, when it all became real, I suddenly realized I couldn't let go. I really couldn't.

I thought I had no ties left in the world, but now I find my attachments are countless!
Most importantly, in this strange land, I can no longer find that sense of belonging that was mine. A sense of belonging...
Deep down, I will always be an outsider...

---

**[SCENE 09]**

Jun Xie quietly closed his eyes, gently tilted his head, and, unseen by anyone, a single tear slid silently down his cheek...
This was the first tear of his two lifetimes!

Men don't easily shed tears, only when they are truly heartbroken!

He stared blankly at the young, almost childish face in the bronze mirror before him. The face was slightly thin, with thin lips and long eyebrows slanting into his temples, making his eyes look somewhat narrow and sharp.

Jun Xie gave a bitter smile and muttered to himself, "I have to say, this guy isn't bad looking. Quite handsome, actually. But isn't he a bit too much of a pretty boy? A bit effeminate?"

Thinking of his previous life, how imposing and fearsome he was!
Even though he wasn't particularly handsome—his eyes were a bit small, a bit narrow, his nose bridge a bit low, and his overall look was a bit too ordinary—he was a real man!

Those pretty boys, even if they had everything a real man had, he still looked down on them. He never imagined that after transmigrating, he'd end up in the body of a standard pretty boy, and a rather good-looking one at that...

"Hey, buddy, are you the one who brought me here?"
His right hand gently stroked a small pagoda-shaped pattern on his left wrist, resembling a tattoo.

A hint of pride surfaced on Jun Xie's face. Even though I've transmigrated, this thing is still in Chinese hands, not fallen into the hands of those foreign devils!

This pagoda-shaped pattern—wasn't it exactly the same as that delicate little pagoda he had fought so desperately for!
Even though it had become a small pattern on his hand, Jun Xie was absolutely certain that this was that pagoda!

He couldn't explain why, but he just had this feeling in his heart. It was solid, yet also mystical.

Seeing this pattern, the only thing that could bring him solace from his past life, Jun Xie's heart churned with turbulent emotions, unable to define what he was feeling.

---

**[SCENE 10]**

But his naturally steady temperament meant his face betrayed nothing.
It remained indifferent! Calm!

Suddenly, the pagoda pattern he was gently stroking emitted a hazy yellow glow. Then Jun Xie suddenly felt dizzy, light-headed. Next, he felt as if something had appeared in his mind, and the pattern on his hand vanished without a trace...

"Strange!" Shaking his head, Jun Xie clicked his tongue in wonder. This thing really is bizarre. First, it went from a palm-sized pagoda to a tattoo on my body, and now it's mysteriously disappeared.

Could this thing actually be some legendary immortal treasure?

"Young Master, the Old Master requests your presence."

Just as Jun Xie was about to investigate what had appeared in his mind, a voice suddenly spoke up.

"Requests my presence?" Jun Xie raised an eyebrow. "For what?"

Why should I go just because the old man asks?
Does he think I'm his grandson?!

He almost blurted this out but swallowed the words. He remembered that the old man was indeed his grandfather, or at least the grandfather of this body...

"I... this servant does not know." The little girl glanced at him fearfully, lowered her head, her long eyelashes blinking nervously. She shifted her weight from one foot to the other, her small body half-turned, ready to bolt at any moment...
