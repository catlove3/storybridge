The Stable Lie (Demo Test Script v0 · 8 Scenes)

【S01 Blind Date Dinner】
A private room in a restaurant. Lin Xiaodong (male lead, 28, a junior employee at a startup) meets Su Wan (female lead) for the first time. Su's mother cuts straight to the chase: "Xiao Lin, where do you work?" Lin Xiaodong: "I do operations at a startup." Su's mother's face darkens: "Not a government job? Without a stable position, how are you supposed to make a living?"

【S02 Su Family Living Room】
Su's father keeps Lin Xiaodong behind alone: "When the Su family marries off a daughter, we don't look at feelings—we look at security. You don't even have a stable post. What can you offer Wanwan for the future?" Lin Xiaodong lowers his head and stays silent.

【S03 The Bride Price Showdown】
Su's parents demand a bride price of 388,000 yuan, "consider it a test of your sincerity and capability." The money Lin Xiaodong has saved is nowhere near enough. He starts borrowing from everyone and taking on side gigs—working his day job, then hustling at night. He loses weight, looking gaunt.

【S04 Misunderstanding Grows】
To raise money, Lin Xiaodong turns down an invitation to a core position on the company's internal startup project. A colleague warns him: "This opportunity won't come again." He says: "I can't wait for the long game right now. I need quick cash." Su Wan notices he's been secretive lately, always working overtime and moonlighting, and mistakenly assumes he isn't serious about their marriage.

【S05 The Breakup】
Su Wan confronts Lin Xiaodong: "Do you even have a plan for our future?" Lin Xiaodong can't bring himself to admit he's been borrowing money to scrape together the bride price (talking about money feels humiliating to him), and only says, "Just give me a little more time." Su Wan, in tears, breaks up with him: "My mom was right. A man without a stable job and without savings can't give anyone a sense of security."

【S06 Separate Paths】
Three years pass. Lin Xiaodong uses the first pot of money he earned from side gigs to co-found a tech company with a partner, focused on local life services. The company is now valued at 200 million yuan in its Series B round. Su Wan, meanwhile, keeps plodding along at her public-sector job. Her family starts setting her up with new prospects—one candidate "has a stable post and a house"—but Su Wan feels something is missing.

【S07 Reunion at the Class Gathering】
A high school reunion. Zhao Ziang, a former rival who got into a top-tier university and now works as a mid-level manager at a big tech firm, teases him publicly: "Xiaodong couldn't even get into a proper workplace back then, right? I hear he's still 'looking for opportunities'?" The crowd bursts into laughter. Su Wan stands up to defend Lin Xiaodong. Lin Xiaodong smiles calmly, not bothering to reveal that he's the founder of the very chain enterprise that just acquired the biggest client company of Zhao Ziang's employer.

【S08 The Identity Reversal】
Su Wan's company is set to discuss a partnership with a tech firm. When the conference room door opens, the person sitting at the head of the table is none other than Lin Xiaodong. Zhao Ziang, who once mocked him, now presents the proposal as the vendor's contact person—only to have Lin Xiaodong casually point out flaws in it. After the meeting, Lin Xiaodong says to Su Wan: "A stable post can give you security, but what I want to give you is something else." Su Wan looks at the projection screen reading "Founder: Lin Xiaodong," and bursts into tears.
